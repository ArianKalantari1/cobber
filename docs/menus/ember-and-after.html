The Violet Leather

Opulent · Smoky · Leather

Cacao-nib–infused Hine cognac45 ml
Toro Albalá 'Don PX'10 ml
Dark crème de cacao5 ml
Roasted blood orange syrup8 ml
Orange bitters2 dash
Lagavulin 16, float5–7 ml

Method Stir all but the float over ice ~30 rotations; strain over one large block in a double old-fashioned. Float the Lagavulin over the back of a spoon; express a blood orange peel and rest it on the block.

Batch ×10 Cacao cognac 450 · Don PX 100 · crème de cacao 50 · blood orange syrup 80 · orange bitters ~10 ml. Undiluted, fine-strained. Keeps 3–6 months. Pour 65–70 ml, stir over ice, float to order.

Why it works Cognac is the hub: it carries vanillin (→ crème de cacao & Lagavulin) and beta-damascenone (→ Don PX & blood orange). The PX is also the body — raisined dissolved solids — and the cognac's chemical soulmate at 0.67.

Prep & Infusions

06

Roasted Blood Orange Syrup Syrup

Roast Halve 4–5 blood oranges, dust with caster (or brown) sugar, 200 °C for 25–30 min until edges char.

Syrup Squeeze + scrape, strain to ~250 ml juice. Combine 1.5 : 1 sugar : juice (rich, for body), warm to dissolve — don't boil. Pinch of salt.

Cardamom gomme For Sanguine: dissolve gum arabic into the warm syrup for silky texture, and steep 2–3 crushed cardamom pods. Strain.

Keeps 2 wks plain; ~6–8 wks at 2:1 + a splash of cognac, bottled hot; indefinite frozen in cubes.

Blood Orange Confit Garnish + Syrup

Method Slow-cook peeled segments in 1:1 syrup at a bare simmer 30–40 min until glossy and translucent.

Use Spoon syrup + a segment into the glass. Richer than the roasted syrup and edible.

Keeps Weeks to a couple of months, segments fully submerged in a sterile sealed jar.

Cacao-Nib Cognac Infusion

Method 40 g roasted cacao nibs per 700 ml Hine. Room temp, 24–48 hr — taste from 24. Fine strain.

Why Chocolate as dry aroma, not sweetness — lets you cut the crème de cacao and keep the drink spirit-forward.

Keeps Shelf-stable for months once strained. Don't leave nibs in past 2 days (goes dusty).

Earl Grey / Lady Grey Cognac Infusion

Method 1 heaped tbsp loose tea per 700 ml cognac, room temp. Taste from 30 min, pull by ~45. Strain at once.

Why Bergamot ties to blood orange; tea tannin adds a leathery grip (structure Cobber can't see — the aroma-only blind spot).

Note Over-steeping turns it harsh & over-tannic. A brighter alternative to the cacao infusion.

Lavender-Honey Syrup Syrup

Method Warm 100 g honey + 100 ml water (don't boil), off heat add 1 tbsp culinary lavender, steep 20 min, strain.

Note Go easy — over-steeped lavender turns soapy. Keeps ~2 wks refrigerated.

Blood Orange / Loquat Shrub Shrub

Method Equal parts fruit + sugar, macerate 24 hr, add equal part vinegar (white wine or apple cider), rest 2–3 days, strain.

Use The bright, tangy body for the spritzes & palomas — never in a spirit-forward stirred drink (the acid fights it).
