# Pisco Ma Chérie: prep spec

Tall strawberry pisco highball for the Signatures, replacing El Diablito. (L'Apéritif Cosmo is also
leaving the menu, as of 2026-10-22.) Background and the tests
behind it are in [pisco-sherry-ideas.md](pisco-sherry-ideas.md).
Named Pisco Ma Chérie on the menu (working title was Pisco, Mon Sherry).
Last updated: 2026-10-22. Not yet tasted as a full build: run the first-tasting checks below.

```
PISCO MA CHÉRIE 23 (price to confirm)
Pisco, Sherry, Strawberry, Black Pepper, Soda
Tall, Juicy, Tangy
```

## 1. Strawberry shrub (about 1.1 L, enough for 4 rail bottles)

**The day before (5 minutes):** into a lidded tub, cool room, 24 hours. Stir once if you pass.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Frozen strawberries | 1 kg |
| White sugar | 400 g |
| Demerara sugar | 200 g |
| Black peppercorns, coarsely cracked (not ground) | 3 g |

**Prep day (15 minutes):**
1. Strain through a sieve, pressing lightly: about 1 L syrup. Bin the pulp and pepper.
2. Whisk in until dissolved:

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Moro Sherry Vinegar (Vinagre de Jerez D.O.P., 250 ml bottle) | 120 ml |
| Citric acid | 18 g |
| Malic acid | 6 g |
| Salt | 1.5 g |

3. Taste 15 ml with 45 ml cold soda. If it's flat, add 3 g citric and 1 g malic and taste again;
   at most twice. (Sherry vinegar is sharper than FORVM, hence the lower start. With FORVM
   Chardonnay instead, start at 21 g citric and 7 g malic.) It should taste like a bright strawberry soda, with no prickle in the nose.
4. Bottle full to the top, label and date. Fridge, and rest 2 days before batching.

**Keeps:** about 3–4 weeks in the fridge without sorbate (not yet tested); freeze half on day 0 if
it sells slowly. Throw it out if it fizzes or hisses when opened, grows a film, or smells yeasty or
boozy.

## 2. Batch (per 700 ml rail bottle)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pisco | 320 ml |
| Elegante Medium sherry (Gonzalez Byass, Summer Hill Wine Shop) | 140 ml |
| Strawberry shrub | 240 ml |

Exactly 700 ml; about 22% ABV. No water. One shrub batch makes 4 rail bottles (1.28 L pisco,
560 ml Medium, 960 ml shrub), with about 140 ml shrub spare.

**Keeps:** safe for well over a month in the cool room, but at its best for about 2 weeks: the red
colour and fresh strawberry aroma fade first. Make 2 weeks' worth at a time, keep it dark. The rail
bottle warms during service, so fill it with one or two shifts' worth and never pour warm leftovers
back into the drum.

## 3. Service

1. Highball filled to the top with cubes.
2. 90 ml batch (a 60 and a 30 jigger). Shake the bottle first if it has settled.
3. 3–4 spins with the bar spoon to chill it.
4. Top with 75 ml cold bottled soda or Purezza sparkling, poured down the spoon. One lift.
5. Garnish: a dehydrated orange wheel stood inside the glass against the side, then one twist of
   black pepper over the top. Both are in stock, need no prep and take seconds. Orange on coral-red
   looks warm and echoes the Medium's amber and the demerara; orange and sherry are a classic pair
   (Cobber tradition 0.36) and orange lifts the cocoa note (chocolate and orange). Runner-up: a
   dehydrated lemon wheel, brighter, and strawberry and lemon is the classic (10 bar drinks); put
   both on the first tasting glass and pick by eye. Skip pineapple and apple (owner overlaps), lime
   (green on red reads Christmas, weakest link to strawberry), and rosemary or thyme (no herbs on
   new signatures).

**Optional visual, at no extra cost:** spread the strained shrub pulp thin on a lined dehydrator
tray (about 5 minutes), dry overnight, and snap it into shards; rest one on the ice. It's waste
already paid for, pepper included. It's sugary, so it goes sticky in humid air: keep it sealed.
(Freeze-dried strawberries are the expensive route; chips cut from fresh punnets are cheap but cost
slicing time and go soft fast.)

About 190 ml, 10.2% ABV, 1.54 standard drinks. 7 full pours per rail bottle.

## 4. Buying

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Elegante Medium sherry | Summer Hill Wine Shop | $25, $19.99 in any six; 140 ml per rail |
| Moro Sherry Vinegar (Vinagre de Jerez D.O.P.), 250 ml | Supermarket vinegar shelf | Cheap; 120 ml per shrub batch. FORVM Chardonnay (about $23–26) is the gentler backup |
| Frozen strawberries, 1 kg | Supermarket | One bag per shrub batch |
| Pisco, demerara, white sugar, black peppercorns, citric, malic, salt, soda | Bar stock | |

Cost: pisco and sherry about $3.40 a drink, shrub about 35–40c with Moro sherry vinegar, soda and
garnish about 20c: roughly $4.00 a drink, assuming frozen strawberries at about $8 a kilo (check the
Moro price).

**Allergens:** sulphites (sherry and vinegar). No nuts; the nutty taste comes from the sherry.

## 5. First-tasting checks

1. The acid taste-up (prep step 3).
2. Soda: 75 ml against 60 ml, and bottled soda against Purezza.
3. Can you name the pisco on the first sip? If not, try 340 ml pisco and 120 ml Medium per rail.
4. Garnish: dehydrated orange against lemon wheel, by eye; is the pepper twist enough on the nose?
5. One comparison glass with ginger ale. If ginger's warmth wins, 20 g sliced fresh ginger replaces
   the pepper in the maceration; the top stays soda.
6. Batch drum on days 7 and 14 against a fresh one: colour and aroma.
