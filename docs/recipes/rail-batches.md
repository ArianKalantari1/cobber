# Rail batches for the signatures (12 November 2026)

Full spec for A Rosy Invitation: [a-rosy-invitation.md](a-rosy-invitation.md).

Olivia is left out for now while the recipe is still being worked on.

Pre-batched signatures that sit on the spirit rail during service and go back to the cool room
overnight. Goal: every service is one jigger pour from the batch plus at most two fresh items.

## House rules (Ari)

- **Rail time: 8 hours out at most per day.** Write the time it went out on the bottle tag. At close
  it goes back to the cool room, capped. Don't top up a used bottle with fresh batch; finish it first.
- **Never in a batch:** citrus juice, open fruit purée, soda or sparkling, olive
  oil, fresh herbs. These are added at service. **Bitters are fine in a batch** (Ari, 12 Nov). **So is an acidified cordial**, as long as the mixed bottle stays at about 20% ABV or more (watermelon cordial in the A Rosy Invitation batch).
- **Sugar may go in the batch only when the batch is high in alcohol** (about 20% ABV or more) and the
  sweetener is a bottled or shelf-stable syrup.
- **Low-ABV batches stay on ice** (Ari, 12 Nov): Sangria Tinto (about 16%) and Pisco Ma Chérie (about 20%, has shrub). Stand the capped bottle in an ice-and-water bucket up to the neck, keep the ice topped up, and change the bucket every shift. The 8-hour rail limit is for the high-ABV bottles kept at room temperature (Summer Fling). A Rosy Invitation can sit in the ice well all service (Ari, 12 Nov), which also slows its flavour and colour fade. Everything goes back to the cool room at close.
- **Saline goes in the batch** (Ari, 22 Nov): any cocktail that needs saline gets it mixed into the batch bottle, not added at service. House Gin Martini 0.1 ml of 20% saline per drink (1 ml per 700 ml bottle), Tomato Martini 0.2 ml per drink (2 ml per bottle), House Vodka Martini 0.1 ml per drink (assumed, confirm). Dirty martinis then just add the brine on top; the saline in the batch is a negligible amount of salt next to 15 ml of brine.
- Fixed jigger pour for every batch, so no measuring at service. Shake the bottle first if it has
  settled. Label: drink, date made, use-by, pour size.
- Batches are 700 ml rail bottles (Sangria Tinto: a 750 ml bottle).

## Dirty Martini Spritz: final spec to bench (18 Nov 2026): $23

Result of the 20-loop run plus review (docs/recipes/dirty-spritz-loop-review.md): the region is firm, the exact numbers are not. The
model has no taste data and the brine salinity is unknown, so **bench the brine ladder before locking it** (12.5, 15, 17.5, 20, 22.5 ml
of brine at a fixed 60 ml batch; pick the lowest that tastes dirty).

Per drink: **20 gin · 22.5 dry vermouth · 17.5 olive brine · 1 dash orange bitters · 50 French sparkling (brut nature or extra brut) ·
20 soda**, built in a chilled wine glass over cubed ice, 3 Green Queen olives on a pick and a lemon twist, lifted once. The first three
are 60 ml: one jigger. About 1.39 standard drinks, about 11.7% ABV in the glass, about $2.76 per drink before garnish (about 13% pour
cost at $23).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small (about 365 ml) | 120 | 135 | 105 | 5 | 6 |
| Standard (about 608 ml) | 200 | 225 | 175 | 8 | 10 |

About 19% ABV. Fill to the top, label the date, best within about 2 weeks (vermouth fades first; untested). Fallbacks: 17.5/22.5/20 batch with
60 sparkling and 30 soda (1.40 std), or 17.5/27.5/15 with 50 sparkling and 20 soda (1.375 std) if the brine tastes salty; the
previous spec was 15/25/20 with 60 sparkling and 20 soda (1.35 std).

### House dirty brine (quick, pasteurised)
150 g drained olives + 150 ml jar brine + 150 ml cold boiled water + 3 g citric acid. Blitz 30-60 s, strain through a fine sieve (then a coffee
filter if wanted), skim any oil, heat the strained liquid to 72 C for 15 s (do not boil), cool in an ice bath, bottle in sanitised bottles
filled to the top, fridge. About 2-3 weeks (estimate, untested); freeze in 20 ml portions for longer. Weigh 100 ml to estimate salinity
(about 103-104 g is roughly 4-6% salt) and taste it against the jar brine. Check keeping times with the food safety supervisor.

Ideas to bench after the brine: Manzanilla 2.5-5 ml (replace 5 ml of vermouth, keep the batch at 60 ml), a Gilda or caperberry garnish,
brine ice, a few drops of 20% saline, olives marinated in the batch bottle.
