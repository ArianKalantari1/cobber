# Infusions for the new cocktails (17 November 2026)

Five house infusions. **On file** means the amounts come from Ari's specs or the costing sheet; **starting point** means I have proposed
amounts that have not been tasted and Ari should confirm. Costs use the retail placeholder prices in the costing sheet (ex GST).

## Rules for every infusion
- Spirit at 37.5-40% ABV (no high-proof). Clean glass jar, rinsed with boiling water; amber or opaque for storage.
- Steep in the cool room, capped, shake once a day, **taste daily** and strain as soon as it tastes right. Over-steeping makes it
  bitter, mushy or medicinal.
- Strain through a fine sieve, then a coffee filter. Re-bottle filled to the top (little air), label with the date and the use-by.
- Taste each batch against the last one; produce varies.

## 1. Cucumber-infused rum (A Rosy Invitation): on file
- **Makes about 680 ml:** 700 ml white rum + 250 g Lebanese cucumber, sliced, peel striped, stem end cut off and binned.
- **Steep:** cool room 2-3 days (the spec's fridge method was 12-24 h; cold is slower). Taste daily; strain once it tastes of cucumber.
- **Keeps:** about 3 weeks (estimate, untested). Taste weekly. Cost about $37.73 per bottle.
- The cucumber masks the cooked note of the pasteurised H2melon, so when it fades the cooked watermelon returns.

## 2. Sun-dried tomato and rosemary gin (Tomato Martini): amounts partly on file
- **On file (costing sheet):** 700 ml gin (2 Pound 10) + 50 g sun-dried tomatoes in oil + half a bunch of rosemary. Steep time is TBC.
- **Starting point:**
  1. Drain the tomatoes and pat off as much oil as you can (oil carries into the gin as a fat wash and can go rancid). Dry-packed
     tomatoes are cleaner.
  2. Steep the tomatoes alone in the gin for **24-48 h** in the cool room.
  3. Add about **10 g rosemary for the last 4-6 hours only**: longer turns resinous and medicinal.
  4. Strain through a sieve and a coffee filter. If it is cloudy, chill it in the fridge for a few hours and filter again to remove oil.
- **Notes:** sun-dried tomatoes are salty, so taste before adding saline in the martini. Use within about 3-4 weeks (estimate).
  Cost about $32.34 per 680 ml.

## 3. Cornichon vodka (Cornichon Gibson): on file
- **On file (costing sheet):** 700 ml house vodka + 100 g cornichons drained from the jar. Cost about $31.30 per 680 ml.
- **Starting point:** slice the cornichons, steep **24-48 h** in the cool room, taste daily, strain through a sieve and a coffee filter.
  It should taste tangy and briny but not harshly acidic, because the drink also gets pickle brine at service.
- **Keeps:** about 4 weeks (estimate). Optional: 2 g fresh dill for the last 6 hours (not on file).

## 4. House spice-infused vodka (Apple Pie Spritz): starting point
Ari has no amounts on file for this. Apple-pie spices, kept to a gentle dose; the vanilla is separate in the drink.
- **Per 700 ml vodka:** 2 cinnamon quills (about 6 g) · 6 allspice berries, cracked · 2 cloves · a pinch (0.5 g) of grated nutmeg.
- **Steep:** cool room **24-36 h**; taste at 12 h. **Take the cloves out at 12-24 h** (they turn medicinal first). Strain through a
  coffee filter.
- **Keeps:** about 6-8 weeks. Cost about $31.5 (the vodka dominates).
- To dose the spice without re-making the batch, make a stronger small tincture and add drops.

## 5. Makrut lime tincture (Kaffi Gimlet): starting point
No amounts on file. It is a tincture, so it is made strong and dosed by the ml.
- **Per 200 ml of 40% vodka:** about 15 g fresh makrut lime leaves (or about 8 g dried), torn, not blended.
- **Steep:** cool room **24-48 h**, taste daily, strain through a coffee filter.
- **Dose:** start at **2-5 ml per drink** and adjust by taste; it is potent. Ari's own dose is not on file.
- **Keeps:** about 6-8 weeks (the aroma fades before it spoils).
