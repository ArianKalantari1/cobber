# Spring Signatures R&D (test sheet + service plan)

Three new signatures for the spring/summer menu, one per base: rum, pisco and tequila.
Test-day specs come first, then the long-life service plan for each drink once it's approved,
then why each choice was made.
Last updated: 2026-10-19.

House rules these follow:
- Every prep keeps about a month (freezing half is fine).
- Service is batch plus at most 2 fresh items.
- No herby or medicinal flavours, nothing Thai, no saffron.
- No owner overlaps: vanilla, passionfruit, pineapple, peach, apple, honey, elderflower,
  Chartreuse.

## Prep for test day

- [ ] **Grapefruit rum.** Start this first; it needs 4 h minimum. Steep the peel of ½ grapefruit
      (Y-peeler, no pith) in 150 ml white rum, then strain.
- [ ] **Blackberry and agave purée** (about 540 ml, 18 drinks):
  1. Boil 500 g frozen blackberries, 100 g sugar and 100 ml Monin Agave for 2 min.
  2. Mash, then sieve out the seeds.
  3. Stir in 2.5 g citric acid and 10 ml red wine vinegar.
  4. Cool covered, then fill squeeze bottles (cut the tip wider).
- [ ] **Guava purée** (about 8 drinks). Drain 1 Koo can and keep the syrup. Blend the halves with
      2 tbsp of the syrup, then sieve. Keeps 3 days in the fridge; freeze the rest in 30 ml cubes.
- [ ] **Macadamia orgeat:**
  1. Blend 80 g lightly toasted macadamias with 160 ml warm macadamia milk, then strain and
     squeeze.
  2. Weigh it and stir in the same weight of sugar, then add 15 ml vodka.
- [ ] **Watermelon juice.** Blend a wedge and sieve it. Keeps 1–2 days.
- [ ] Optional: **dried grapefruit wheels.** Slice 3 mm thick, dry at 80 °C fan-forced for
      4–6 h and flip once. Store airtight.
- [ ] Check stock:
  - Spirits and liqueurs: Lillet Rosé, Cointreau, absinthe, Aperol, crème de mûre.
  - Bitters and Tajín: fire bitters, Angostura, Tajín.
  - Wine and juice: rescued Sauvignon Blanc, pomegranate juice.

| Guava purée | 30 ml |
| Aperol | 10 ml |
| Sauvignon Blanc | 10 ml |
| Lime | 20 ml |

## 3. Blackberry Tommy's (tequila and mezcal), rocks glass with a Tajín rim

| What goes in | Per drink |

| Fire bitters | 2 dashes |

## Service plan (once approved)

### Watermelon Reviver

**Spirit batch: 700 ml, 11 drinks.** Keep it in the fridge because of the Lillet; it lasts about
2 months.
- Ingredients: 230 ml white rum, 230 ml Lillet Rosé, 230 ml Cointreau, 10 ml absinthe and 1 ml
  20% saline (20 drops).
- Steep the peel of 1 grapefruit in the batch for 24 h, then strain.

**Watermelon** keeps 6+ weeks in the fridge either way:
- **H2melon cordial.** Stir 30 g sugar, 2 g citric, 1 g malic and 0.2 g potassium sorbate into
  500 ml H2melon (Woolworths, Dan Murphy's). Pour 30 ml. Taste H2melon next to fresh juice first,
  because it's pasteurised.
- **Homemade clarified cordial.**
  - Boil 1.25 g agar in 125 ml water for 2 min.
  - Whisk it into 500 ml room-temperature juice, let it set, then drain through a nut-milk bag.
  - Stir in the same sugar, acids and sorbate as the H2melon cordial. Pour 40 ml.
  - It comes out pale, but the Lillet Rosé keeps the drink pink.

**Per drink:** 60 ml batch, the watermelon (30 or 40 ml) and 20 ml lemon.

**Backup fruit: pomegranate** (Bickford's or Sunraysia 100% juice).
- When you open the bottle, add 0.4 g potassium sorbate per litre; it then keeps 6+ weeks.
- Per drink: 60 ml batch, 25 ml pomegranate and 20 ml lemon.

### Guava and Macadamia Pisco

- **Guava purée:** freeze it in 30 ml cubes, which keep for months, and thaw a few days' worth at
  a time. Koo cans sell out, so buy ahead.
- **If Koo cans are unavailable:** use 45 ml guava nectar per drink. For service,
  freeze-concentrate the nectar: freeze it, keep the first half of the melt, and use 30 ml.
- **Orgeat:** add 0.2 g potassium sorbate for 4–6 weeks in the fridge, and freeze half.
- **Batch:** set the spec after the test. Pisco, Aperol, Sauvignon Blanc and saline batch well;
  the guava, orgeat and lime go in at service.

### Blackberry Tommy's

**Batch: 700 ml, 11 drinks.** It keeps for months with no fridge needed.
- Ingredients: 525 ml tequila, 115 ml mezcal and 60 ml crème de mûre.
- Add fire bitters at your test dash count × 11, about 0.8 ml per dash.

**Purée:** as above. Cooked, it keeps about 4 weeks in the fridge. An uncooked thaw-and-mash
version only keeps about 10 days. One 500 g bag makes about 18 drinks.

**Per drink:** 60 ml batch, 30 ml purée and 25 ml lime. Shake and dump.

## Why these choices

### Watermelon Reviver
- **Rum:**
  - The only rum on the menu is the Old Fashioned option.
  - In Ari's Sydney dataset, none of the 11 watermelon drinks use rum; 6 of them are tequila.
  - Hawksmoor's Double Melon Daiquiri (rum, melon, rosé) shows the pairing works.
  - It keeps the drink clear of The Charles Bar's pisco Watermelon Crush.
- **Cobber scores for the base:** how often each base appears with the Reviver's other ingredients
  in recipes. Gin 0.63, rum 0.48, pisco 0.03. Dry vermouth stands in for Lillet.
- **Lillet Rosé and grapefruit:** red-berry notes and colour. Rum and grapefruit score 0.56 in
  Cobber.
- **Pomegranate as the backup:**
  - Cobber's best fit with the Reviver's other ingredients: an average of 0.50, including 0.70
    with rum and 0.77 with lemon.
  - Only 3 of the 265 Sydney drinks use it.

### Guava and Macadamia Pisco
- **Sauvignon Blanc:** it shares guava's tropical sulphur compounds (3-sulfanylhexyl acetate and
  3-sulfanylhexanol).
- **Angostura:** its spice echoes guava's cinnamon note (cinnamyl acetate).
- **Small orgeat pour:** the fat in orgeat mutes fruit aroma.
- **Aperol at 10 ml:** cut down so it doesn't cover the guava.

### Blackberry Tommy's
- **Blackberry:**
  - None of the 45 Sydney tequila and mezcal drinks use it.
  - It avoids Bopp & Tone's spicy mango margarita and Papa Gede's Dancing Devil (tequila, mezcal,
    spiced pear, jalapeño).
  - Berries suit the vanilla note in the house tequila.
- **Fruits ruled out:** cherry stays with the cherry Shiraz cobbler, and El Diablito comes off the
  menu.
- **Fire bitters:** they replace jalapeño, because no pepperberry could be found.

### Preps in general
- Clarity isn't what keeps a prep safe; sugar, acid and heat are.
