# A Rosy Invitation (menu name A.R.I.): full spec (12 November 2026)

Corpse Reviver twist: white rum infused with cucumber, Lillet Rosé, Cointreau, absinthe, watermelon-rose
concentrate and lemon. Status: **test build, not yet tasted.** The cucumber masks the cooked note of the
pasteurised H2melon.

## 1. Cucumber rum (700 ml rum, about 680 ml out)

- 700 ml white rum + 250 g Lebanese cucumber, sliced, peel striped, **stem end cut off and binned**.
- Cool room, capped, opaque or amber bottle. **Taste daily; strain at day 2-3** once it tastes of cucumber
  (sieve, then coffee filter). Return to a clean bottle, filled to the top, dated.
- Use within about 3 weeks. Taste weekly to find when it starts to dull (untested).

## 2. Watermelon-rose concentrate (monthly; 2 bottles of H2melon, about 1.15 L, 4 portions of 250 ml)

H2melon: watermelon juice 99.5% + cane juice 0.5%, never from concentrate, 9.5 g sugar and 40 mg sodium
per 100 ml, no preservative, no added acid. Once opened it keeps 3 days: **freeze it the day you open it.**

1. Shake the bottle well. Pour about 125 ml off each 1.25 L bottle; freeze capped for 24 h.
2. Thaw upside down over a jug in the cool room (12-20 h). Keep the first melt, about 550 ml per bottle,
   and bin the pale ice. Don't filter it hard (the red colour is in the cloudy particles).
3. Stir into the cold concentrate (about 1.1 L):

| Ingredient | Per 100 ml | For 1.1 L |
|---|---|---|
| Citric acid | 1.5 g | 16.5 g |
| Malic acid | 0.75 g | 8.25 g |
| Salt (half dose: the juice already has sodium) | 0.05 g | 0.55 g |
| Potassium sorbate, 10% solution (added last) | 0.8 ml | 8.8 ml |
| Rose syrup | 5 ml | 55 ml |

No vitamin C. If the colour is pale, add the hibiscus shot (2 g dried hibiscus in 30 ml just-boiled water,
5 min, strained and cooled) a few ml at a time.

4. Label "contains preservative 202" and the date. Freeze in 250 ml portions. Thaw one in the fridge each
   week. Pasteurised H2melon is the safe base; do not swap in fresh juice without a heat step.

## 3. Weekly batch (10 drinks, 850 ml, use a 1 L bottle)

| In the bottle | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cucumber rum | 245 ml |
| Lillet Rosé | 195 ml |
| Cointreau | 150 ml |
| Absinthe | 12 ml |
| Watermelon-rose concentrate (one 250 ml portion) | 250 ml |

About 23% ABV. Shake before pouring. Rail 8 hours at most, then back to the cool room. Use within about a
week (untested). **Before the first batch, run the overnight haze test** (25 ml concentrate + 60 ml of the
spirit mix, clear glass, fridge): if it hazes or strings, keep the concentrate out of the batch and add
25 ml at service instead.

## 4. One cocktail

- **85 ml batch** (a 60 and a 25 jigger) = 24.5 rum, 19.5 Lillet Rosé, 15 Cointreau, 1.2 absinthe, 25 concentrate
- **+ 10 ml fresh lemon** (cut to 5 ml if too sharp; the concentrate carries acid equal to about 9 ml lemon)
- Shake hard on ice, double strain into a chilled coupe.
- About 1.5 standard drinks.
