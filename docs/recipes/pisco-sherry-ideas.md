# Pisco and sherry: five more drinks

Other drinks on La Pasión's frame (pisco the hero, sherry for depth), with a different fruit
each time. La Pasión holds the spring pisco slot, so these are swaps if it doesn't win, or for
later seasons. None has been tasted.
Last updated: 2026-10-21.

They follow the house rules in [spring-signatures-rnd.md](spring-signatures-rnd.md): no fruit the
menu already owns (peach, apple, raspberry, cassis, grapefruit, pineapple, apricot, passionfruit,
cherry, and the cobblers' pear, plum and pomegranate), no egg white, no Old Fashioned format,
service is a batch plus at most 2 fresh items, and preps keep about a month (freezing half is
fine). Each one keeps pisco at 40 ml and the sherry at 15–20 ml. The numbers come from the same
maths as La Pasión's loop; the acid in Davidson plum and verjuice is an estimate, so balance those
by taste.

| # | Drink | Fruit | Sherry | Glass | Colour | Service |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fresas con Crema | Strawberry | Elegante Cream | Highball | Pink, naturally | Batch, cordial, lemon |
| 2 | Ciruela | Davidson plum (native) | Elegante Medium | Highball | Magenta-pink, naturally | Batch, cordial, lemon |
| 3 | Tres Uvas | Verjuice (grape) | Manzanilla | Highball | Pale straw | Batch only |
| 4 | Mango con Sal | Mango | Manzanilla | Highball | Golden | Batch, mango, lime |
| 5 | Sidecar de Jerez | Orange (Cointreau) | Elegante Medium | Coupe | Amber | Batch, lemon |

## 1. Fresas con Crema (strawberries and cream)

The most approachable of the five, and pink with no colouring. The cream sherry is the "cream".

- **Per drink:** 40 ml pisco, 20 ml Elegante Cream, 30 ml strawberry cordial, 20 ml lemon. Shake
  5–8 seconds, dump into a highball, fresh cubes, top with 70 ml soda.
- **Batch:** pisco and Elegante Cream 2:1, 60 ml a drink.
- **Strawberry cordial:** 1 kg frozen strawberries and 500 g white sugar, covered in the fridge for
  24 hours (thawing draws the juice out), then strained without pressing hard. No cooking, so it
  tastes fresh, not jammy. About 1.1 L; 2 weeks in the fridge, freeze half.
- **Numbers:** about 200 ml, 9.6% ABV, 1.5 standard drinks, sugar-to-acid about 12:1.
- **Why:** Cobber gives strawberry and sherry real shared chemistry (0.29) but only one bar drink
  (Pretty In Pink), so it's novel; strawberry and lemon is classic (10 drinks).

## 2. Ciruela (Davidson plum)

The most novel, and the native one. Davidson plum is intensely sour and deep magenta, so the
same fruit gives colour and tartness. Its dark-plum note suits the Medium's oxidised, dried-fruit
side.

- **Per drink:** 40 ml pisco, 15 ml Elegante Medium, 25 ml Davidson plum cordial, 15 ml lemon.
  Shake, dump, fresh cubes, top with 80 ml soda. Taste before adding the lemon: the plum may
  carry enough acid on its own.
- **Batch:** pisco and Elegante Medium, 40:15 (55 ml a drink).
- **Davidson plum cordial:** 500 g frozen Davidson plums, 500 g sugar and 250 ml water, warmed
  until the fruit collapses (about 10 minutes), blended briefly and fine-strained. About 900 ml;
  add 2 ml sorbate stock and it keeps a month.
- **Numbers:** about 195 ml, 9.6% ABV, 1.5 standard drinks, about 11:1.
- **Why:** Cobber scores Davidson plum at 0.30 novelty with both pisco and sherry and finds no bar
  drinks with either: the least-trodden pairing here. Its profile is provisional.
- **Buying:** Bush to Bowl, Terrey Hills, sells frozen 500 g packs for pickup. The fruit is in
  season November to February, so stock up then; freeze-dried powder is the backup.

## 3. Tres Uvas (three grapes)

The simplest service on the menu: one pour and soda. Pisco is grape brandy, manzanilla is grape
wine and verjuice is unripe grape juice, so the whole drink is grape. Best with an aromatic pisco.

- **Per drink:** 107.5 ml batch over cubes in a highball, top with 75 ml soda, one lift with a bar
  spoon. A frozen white grape on a pick if you want a garnish.
- **Batch (6 drinks, 645 ml):** 240 ml pisco, 90 ml manzanilla, 270 ml Maggie Beer verjuice,
  45 ml simple syrup (1:1). About 17% ABV. The manzanilla fades first, so use it within 2 weeks.
- **Numbers:** about 190 ml, 9.5% ABV, 1.4 standard drinks, about 10:1. Dry and crisp, like a
  wine spritz. If it's too sharp, add simple 2.5 ml a drink at a time.
- **Why:** Cobber can't score this one: its pisco, sherry and grape profiles are placeholders.
- **Buying:** Maggie Beer Verjuice at Woolworths, about 10.5 g sugar per 100 g. It contains
  sulphites, as sherry does.

## 4. Mango con Sal

Summer's crowd-pleaser: mango with the manzanilla's salt. Mango is tropical, like La Pasión, so
pick one or the other for a menu, not both.

- **Per drink:** 40 ml pisco, 15 ml manzanilla, 50 g Kesar mango pulp, 15 ml lime. Shake, dump,
  fresh cubes, top with 60 ml soda. No syrup: the pulp is already sweetened.
- **Batch:** pisco and manzanilla, 40:15 (55 ml a drink). Optional: 3 ml 20% saline per 700 ml if
  the manzanilla's salt doesn't come through.
- **Mango:** canned Kesar mango pulp from an Indian grocer (850 g; Kashish or KTC have 20–23 g
  sugar per 100 g and citric acid). Once opened, about 5 days in the fridge; freeze it in
  portions. No chilli: Tajín is C'est Chamoy's.
- **Numbers:** about 205 ml, 8.9% ABV, 1.4 standard drinks, about 10:1.
- **Why:** mango and lime is well trodden (10 bar drinks in Cobber); sherry and mango has one
  (Jardin).

## 5. Sidecar de Jerez

A Sidecar with the base split between pisco and the Medium: orange and nut, no egg. Everything
but the lemon is shelf-stable, so the batch keeps for months.

- **Per drink:** 77.5 ml batch and 20 ml lemon, shaken hard, fine-strained into a coupe.
- **Batch (9 drinks, 700 ml):** 360 ml pisco, 135 ml Elegante Medium, 135 ml Cointreau, 70 ml
  simple syrup (1:1).
- **Numbers:** about 120 ml, 20% ABV, 1.9 standard drinks, about 7:1 (Sidecar-tart).
- **Why:** sherry and orange liqueur is a classic pair in Cobber (0.38: the Up to Date and the
  Chaplin), and pisco and lemon has 48 bar drinks.
- **Overlap:** Cointreau and lemon are also in the Jasmine; the Classics already have a pisco sour
  (with egg).

## Bonus: Pisco Bamboo (Martini Corner)

No fruit, but it fits the Martini Corner, where every drink already uses manzanilla: 30 ml pisco,
30 ml manzanilla, 15 ml dry vermouth, 2 dashes orange bitters, stirred. Batch with 20 ml water a
serve and keep it in the fridge. Sherry and dry vermouth is Cobber's strongest sherry pairing
(0.48, the Bamboo).

## Strawberry and mango, more sophisticated

Two drinks per fruit: a long signature and a stirred one for the Martini Corner. Each uses at
most 3 fresh ingredients when batching (here 1–2), and service stays a batch plus at most 2
fresh items. Fresa y Jerez and Mango Asado share one rail batch: pisco and Elegante Medium,
40:15, 55 ml a drink (480 ml pisco and 180 ml Medium per 700 ml bottle, 12 drinks).

| Drink | Format | Fresh at batching | The idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresa y Jerez | Highball | Strawberries, lemons | Sherry two ways: the wine in the batch, the vinegar in a shrub |
| Fresa Martini | Stirred | Strawberries | Strawberry pisco and manzanilla; native strawberry gum repeats strawberry's own aroma |
| Mango Asado | Highball | Mangoes, limes | Charred mango, so the caramel meets the Medium's toasted caramel |
| Mango y Manzanilla | Stirred | Mangoes | Mango pisco, manzanilla salt and a dash of spice |

### Fresa y Jerez (strawberry and sherry)

- **Per drink:** 55 ml pisco–Medium batch, 30 ml strawberry shrub, 10 ml lemon. Shake 5–8
  seconds, dump into a highball, fresh cubes, top with 80 ml soda.
- **Strawberry shrub (about 710 ml, 23 drinks):** 500 g hulled strawberries (fresh in season, or
  frozen), 350 g sugar and the peel of 2 lemons, covered in the fridge for 24 hours. Strain
  without pressing, lift out the peel, then stir in 120 ml sherry vinegar (Vinagre de Jerez, 7%
  acidity). Sugar and vinegar keep it a month in the fridge. The same trick as strawberries with
  balsamic, using the vinegar made from sherry.
- **Numbers:** about 195 ml, 9.6% ABV, 1.5 standard drinks, 13.8:1 (La Pasión's balance).
  Rosy coral.
- **Why:** strawberry's strongest flavour family partner in Cobber's recipe corpus is
  roasted-nutty (NPMI +0.46, above chance), so the nuttiest sherry, not fino. Strawberry and
  lemon is classic (tradition 0.26, 10 drinks).

### Fresa Martini

- **Per serve:** 97.5 ml from the fridge, poured into a chilled glass. No stirring at service.
- **Batch (7 serves, 700 ml rail):** 315 ml strawberry pisco, 140 ml manzanilla, 70 ml dry
  vermouth, 17.5 ml simple syrup, 140 ml water. About 22% ABV, 1.7 standard drinks, dry (about 2 g
  sugar per 100 ml). Use within 2 weeks: the manzanilla fades first.
- **Strawberry pisco:** 300 g sliced strawberries in 700 ml pisco, fridge, 48 hours, then strain
  without pressing (pressing clouds it). Pale pink; it drifts towards salmon over weeks.
- **Optional native accent:** 1 g ground dried strawberry gum leaf in the strawberry pisco for
  its last 6–12 hours; taste at 6. Strawberry gum's oil is mostly methyl cinnamate, the
  fruity-balsamic note in strawberries themselves (Cobber harmony 0.50), so it reads as more
  strawberry, not eucalyptus. If it tastes minty or medicinal, it's out (house rule). Warndu sells
  whole dried leaf all year; FISH sells 30 g ground for $15.

### Mango Asado (charred mango)

- **Per drink:** 55 ml pisco–Medium batch, 50 ml charred mango cordial, 15 ml lime. Shake, dump,
  fresh cubes, top with 60 ml soda.
- **Charred mango cordial (about 1.45 L, 29 drinks):** cheek 4 ripe mangoes (about 1 kg flesh).
  Char them flesh-down in a dry, very hot pan until blackened in patches (3–4 minutes), then scoop
  the flesh. Blend with 400 g sugar, 100 ml lime juice, 300 ml water and 2 g salt, fine-strain, add
  2 ml sorbate stock. About a month in the fridge; freeze half.
- **Off season:** 850 g canned Kesar pulp with burnt sugar instead of the char (the method of
  La Pasión's old burnt-sugar syrup: take the sugar to medium amber, then whisk in the lime and
  water before blending in the pulp). Cut the sugar to about 330 g; the pulp is sweetened.
- **Numbers:** about 205 ml, 9.1% ABV, 1.5 standard drinks, 14.3:1. Golden amber.
- **Why:** the char's caramel meets the Medium's toasted caramel; the pinch of salt follows
  mango's savoury family in Cobber (NPMI +0.10, above chance). Mango and lime is well trodden
  (10 bar drinks).

### Mango y Manzanilla

- **Per serve:** 97.5 ml from the fridge into a chilled glass.
- **Batch (7 serves, 700 ml rail):** 315 ml mango pisco, 140 ml manzanilla, 70 ml dry vermouth,
  17.5 ml simple syrup, 6 ml Angostura, 1 ml 20% saline, 140 ml water. About 22% ABV, 1.7 standard
  drinks, dry. Use within 2 weeks.
- **Mango pisco:** 300 g diced ripe mango in 700 ml pisco, fridge, 48 hours, then strain through
  a fine sieve and a coffee filter. Pale gold.
- **Why:** savoury is mango's strongest usable family in Cobber; mango has bar-drink support with
  dry vermouth (White Lies, Mango y Machado) and Angostura (Mango No. 3).

**Mango and nut allergies:** mango is in the cashew family, like the pink peppercorn the bar
ruled out. Cross-reactions with cashew or pistachio are reported but inconsistent, and mango flesh
is usually tolerated, so it isn't a nut, but staff should know if a guest with a cashew or
pistachio allergy asks.

**Seasons:** strawberries are at their best in Sydney through spring; Kensington Pride mangoes run
November to March.
