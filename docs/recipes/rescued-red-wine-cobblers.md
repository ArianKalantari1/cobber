# Rescued Red Wine Cobblers: House Spiced Red + Fruit Variants

Status: **R&D**. Run quarter batches, taste on day 3, then scale up.
Last updated: 2026-10-14.
White version and shared method notes: [rescued-white-wine-cobblers.md](rescued-white-wine-cobblers.md).

Leftover soft reds are cooked down with a house spice base and one fruit, sweetened with a
lemon-husk oleo, then fortified into a ready-to-pour **cobbler batch** (~18%). Big tannic
reds go uncooked into the **House Rosso** for the Negroni batch instead (section 7).

---

## 1. Which reds go where

| Red wine | Goes to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Soft, fruity (Pinot, Grenache, Gamay, Merlot, Sangiovese) | **Red cobbler** (cooked) | Reduction concentrates tannin; soft reds stay smooth |
| Big, tannic or oaky (Shiraz, Cab, Malbec) | **House Rosso** (not cooked) | No reduction, so the tannin doesn't concentrate; Campari and sugar cover it |

- Taste every bottle. Discard vinegary or corked wine. Slightly tired wine is fine for the
  rosso (it reads as "aged"), not for the cobbler.
- Use within 3 days, or freeze in 500 ml tubs.
- Don't fortify wine you plan to cook: the heat boils the vodka off.

## 2. What makes it ours (not the old bar's spiced redux)

| | Old bar card | House red |
|---|---|---|
| Spice | 3 cinnamon quills, 3 star anise, 10 cloves, 3 orange zests | 8 allspice, 15 g ginger, 1 cinnamon quill, peel of 2 oranges (the spice set of Caribbean sorrel) |
| Tartness | Rhubarb | Citric to pH 3.3 or below (clean, no flavour). Hibiscus optional, for pale or soft reds |
| Sweetness | 450 g plain sugar | Lemon-husk oleo, off the heat |
| Fruit | None | Four variants (section 4) |
| Finish | Unfortified, 2–3 weeks | Vodka, saline, pH 3.3 or below, a bitters pattern: ~18%, 2–3 months |

Allspice tastes of clove, cinnamon and nutmeg in one berry, so the clove note stays without
10 cloves turning medicinal.

## 3. Method (per 3 L soft red)

**Base, all variants:**

| Part | Full batch (3 L wine) | Quarter test (750 ml wine) |
|---|---|---|
| Spices, simmered from the start | 8 allspice berries, cracked · 15 g fresh ginger, sliced · 1 cinnamon quill · peel of 2 oranges, no pith, **then juice those 2 oranges into the pot** (~150–200 ml; no extra wine or vodka needed, it reduces with the pot) | 2 allspice · 4 g ginger · a quarter of a cinnamon quill · peel and juice of ½ orange |
| Hibiscus, off the heat (**optional**: pale or soft reds only; skip for Shiraz and other big reds) | 10 g dried (about 5 teabags), lid on, 5 min | 2.5 g (1–2 teabags) |
| Oleo | 36 squeezed lemon halves + sugar (per variant) | 9 lemon halves + sugar (per variant) |
| Spirit | 1.4 L vodka (200 ml of it for the husk rinse) | 350 ml vodka (50 ml for the husk rinse) |
| Bitters | Per pattern (section 5) | Per pattern |
| 20% saline | 4 ml | 1 ml |
| Citric | To pH 3.3 or below | To pH 3.3 or below |

1. **Simmer** the wine with the spices, uncovered. Add the fruit at the time given in
   section 4. Reduce until the whole pot, fruit juice included, is down to **1.5 L**.
2. **Off the heat:** if using hibiscus, steep it (lid on, 5 min) and remove it. Stir in the strained
   oleo syrup until any sugar dissolves. **Never cook the husks.**
3. **Fine strain** (press the fruit hard), cool, fine strain again.
4. **Husk rinse** with 200 ml of the vodka, strain it in, then add the rest of the vodka,
   the bitters and the saline.
5. **Citric** to pH 3.3 or below. The orange juice (and hibiscus, if used) add some acid, so check with a
   pH strip first. (The juice adds only ~20 g sugar, too little to change the oleo.)
6. **Bottle and label:** variant, bitters pattern, date, and **"HH 60 / Normal 100"**.

**Result:** about 3.3 L at 16–18%. Safe for 2–3 months in the fridge; best within 6 weeks.
Strength check, shelf life by strength and spoilage signs: see section 3 of the
[white spec](rescued-white-wine-cobblers.md).

## 4. Fruit variants

| Variant | Fruit (full / quarter) | Add when | Oleo sugar (full / quarter) | Bitters pattern | Garnish |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cherry** (lead) | 1 kg frozen pitted cherries / 250 g | Last 20 min | 350 g / 90 g | Bright | Cherry + orange wheel |
| **Plum** | 2 cans SPC plums in juice, stones out (or 1 kg fresh dark plums, Dec–Mar) / ½ can | Whole simmer | 300 g / 75 g | Warm | Orange wheel |
| **Pomegranate** | 150 ml pomegranate molasses / 40 ml. If using hibiscus, halve it: the molasses is already tart | Off the heat, with the oleo | 400 g / 100 g | Fresh | Lemon wheel |
| **Blackberry** | 1 kg frozen blackberries / 250 g | Last 20 min | 400 g / 100 g | Split | Blackberry + lemon wheel |

- Cherries add about 130 g of their own sugar per kg, and canned plums add more through the
  juice, hence the lower oleo sugar.
- Pomegranate adds its own tannin: soft reds only.
- Plum pectin softens tannin: the most forgiving variant if a bigger red slips in.

**Cherry: 1 L base + 60 ml tests (fortify only what you test, to save vodka):**

| Base part (per 1 L red; makes ~700 ml, unfortified) | Amount |
|---|---|
| Wine | 1 L, reduced to 500 ml **before** the fruit goes in, so the dipstick reads true |
| From the start | 3 allspice berries, cracked · 5 g fresh ginger, sliced · 3 cm cinnamon quill · peel of ⅔ orange (4 strips, no pith), then its juice (~60 ml) |
| Cherries | 330 g frozen pitted: add at the 500 ml mark, lid on, gentle simmer 15–20 min |
| Hibiscus | Optional: skip for Shiraz. For pale or soft reds, 2 teabags, off the heat, lid on, 5 min |
| Oleo | 12 squeezed lemon halves + 120 g sugar, stirred in off the heat |
| 20% saline | 1.3 ml (~25 drops), stirred into the base |

Strain hard, cool, strain again. Keeps up to 1 week in the fridge unfortified, or freeze it
in ice-cube trays. Shiraz is a big red: simmer gently.

**60 ml test:** 34 ml base + 26 ml vodka + one layer = ~16–17%, ~135 g/L sugar, the same as a
full batch. Rule for any size: **vodka = ¾ of the base volume**.

| Layer | Per 60 ml test (1 drop ≈ 0.05 ml; use a dropper or 1 ml syringe) |
|---|---|
| Peychaud's (Bright) | 0.5 ml (10 drops) |
| Angostura (Warm) | 0.5 ml (10 drops) |
| Split | 7 drops Peychaud's + 4 drops Angostura |
| Maraschino + Angostura | 1.1 ml + 6 drops |
| Absinthe | 1–2 drops |
| Campari | 1.6 ml |
| Sweet vermouth | 4 ml, on top |

Check one sample with a pH strip: if it reads above 3.3, add citric to the base, 0.3 g at a time.

## 5. Bitters patterns

| Pattern | Full batch | Per 100 ml sample | Character | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bright** | 30 ml Peychaud's | 0.9 ml | Anise, floral | Cherry–Peychaud's 0.36 shared / 0.34 classic |
| **Warm** | 30 ml Angostura | 0.9 ml | Clove, cinnamon | Angostura shares 0.38–0.40 with allspice, clove and cinnamon |
| **Fresh** | 30 ml orange bitters + 10 ml Angostura | 0.9 + 0.3 ml | Peel, lifted | Pomegranate–orange bitters 0.36, pomegranate–Angostura 0.52 classic |
| **Split** | 20 ml Peychaud's + 10 ml Angostura | 0.6 + 0.3 ml | Sazerac-style, in between | Peychaud's lifts, Angostura grounds |

**No Peychaud's?** Per 100 ml of batch: Angostura 0.9 ml (Warm) · maraschino 1.8 ml + Angostura
0.5 ml (cherry–maraschino 0.69 shared, Shiraz–maraschino 0.18) · absinthe 2–4 drops for
Peychaud's anise (the Remember the Maine pairing) · Campari 2.7 ml for a bitter-orange
backbone (cherry–Campari 0.26 classic) · sweet vermouth 7 ml for depth, on top of the vodka,
never instead of it (cherry–sweet vermouth 0.53 classic).

## 6. Service

Same pours as the whites:

- **HH 60 built:** 60 batch, 1 lemon + 1 lime slice lightly muddled, cubes, 20–30 soda, straw.
- **Normal 100 shaken:** 100 batch + 25 lemon + a half-wheel, shake 6–8 s, dump into a
  soda-chilled wine glass, 2–3 cubes + 20 soda.

| Drink | Spec (ml) | Std drinks | Best with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red Kalimotxo | Batch + cola only, ice. Medium glass (HH): 60 batch · top 90 cola (2:3, ~6.8%). Long glass (Normal): 120 batch · 120 cola (1:1, the traditional Kalimotxo ratio, ~8.5%). Dose the batch acid to the long 1:1 | 0.8 / 1.6 | Cherry, plum |
| Red Whiskey Sour | 45 bourbon · 45 batch · 20 lemon, shake, rocks | 2.05 | Cherry, blackberry |
| Red Manhattan | 45 bourbon · 45 batch, stir, coupe (pre-batch with 20 water per serve) | 2.05 | Cherry, plum |
| Red Negroni | 30 gin · 30 Campari · 30 batch | 1.9 | Cherry, pomegranate. Owner's call (Negroni family) |
| Winter hot serve | 100 batch + 50 hot water, heated to ~65°C (never boil), orange wheel | 1.4 | All |

## 7. House Rosso (big reds → the Negroni batch)

Per 3 L rescued red (big reds fine). Makes about **4.5 L at ~16.5%** with ~133 g/L sugar,
close to a commercial rosso: swap it 1:1 into the 30 ml Negroni pour (~150 Negronis per batch).

| Part | Amount |
|---|---|
| Spice tincture (24–48 h, room temp, then strain) | 900 ml vodka · peel of 3 oranges, no pith · 2 cinnamon quills · 2 star anise · 4 cloves |
| Caramel syrup | 300 g sugar cooked dry to dark amber; off the heat carefully add 200 ml **juice from the 3 peeled oranges** (or hot water). It spits, so pour slowly. Stir in another 300 g sugar, then strain |
| Bitter backbone | 30 ml Angostura (gentian-based) |
| Wine | 3 L rescued red, strained |
| Citric | 0–2 g, only if it tastes flat |

1. Make the tincture and strain it.
2. Make the caramel syrup and cool it.
3. Mix wine, tincture, caramel syrup and Angostura. Taste.
4. Bottle and refrigerate. Usable on day 1, better after 3–7 days. Keeps 2–3 months.

- Classic upgrade: add 3 g dried wormwood + 1 g gentian root to the tincture.
- Need it today: steep the spices in 500 ml of the wine at about 70°C, lid on, 20 min,
  instead of the 48 h tincture.
- Consistency: keep one big container and top it up with each new batch (solera-style).
