so check before you buy.
Last updated: 2026-10-21.

## Summer Hill Wine Shop

- 7 Lackey Street, Summer Hill NSW 2130 · (02) 9798 7282 ·
  [summerhillwineshop.com.au](https://www.summerhillwineshop.com.au/)
- Independent and family-run since 1986. Wine only: no beer or spirits. Ships within Australia.
- Fortified wines online: port, Tokay (Topaque), Muscat and sherry (18 sherries). No Madeira or
  amontillado was listed.

## Wholesale: Adamance

- **Passionfruit purée:** $95 for 4 × 1 kg ($23.75/kg), unsweetened, pasteurised and seedless.
  About 65c of purée per La Pasión drink, and 4 kg makes about 150 drinks. Keep the tubs frozen
  and thaw one at a time in the fridge.
- **Apricot purée:** $21/kg. Canned apricots are several times cheaper for the chamoy.

## Specialty

- **Roasted ground wattleseed:** Herbie's Spices, or online (for example Australian Superfood Co,
  30 g).
- **Lúcuma powder:** health-food shops.
- **Dried purple corn:** Latin grocers.
