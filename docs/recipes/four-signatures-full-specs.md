## 1. SUMMER FLING ($25)

*Tequila & Mezcal, Floc de Gascogne, Apricot, Lime & Agave. Fruity, spicy & juicy.*

### Apricot purée (one 825 g can, about 600-650 ml, about 20 drinks)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Canned apricot halves | all the fruit (about 480 g) |
| Can syrup | about 170 g (half): drain all the syrup into a jug, weigh 170 g back into the blender jug, discard the rest |
| Malic acid | 6 g |
| Citric acid | 3 g |
| Salt | 1 g |
| Potassium sorbate, 10% solution | 5 ml |

Blend everything on high for about 1 minute. No sieve. Wide-tip squeeze bottle, labelled "contains
preservative 202", "Made [date] · Use by [date + 10 days]". Vitamin C optional; skip it for the first batch.
Aim for pH 3.5 or below (cheap pH strips). In service, keep the bottle in the esky in ice up to the neck,
top up the ice, and back in the fridge at close; if the ice has gone, don't keep it past that shift. Bin it
if it fizzes, smells yeasty or boozy, or grows mould. Freeze flat in 300 ml bags (10 drinks) on the day you
make it; 2-3 months frozen; thaw in the fridge and never refreeze; fridge days before freezing count toward
the 10. Cans per week = drinks sold / 20.

### Batch (700 ml bottle, about 668 ml, 11 pours of 60)

| In the bottle | Amount |
|---|---|
| Blanco tequila | 380 |
| Mezcal | 95 |
| Floc de Gascogne | 140 |
| Agave nectar, straight | 45 |
| Chilli bitters | 8 (about 1 dash per pour; taste and adjust) |

About 33% ABV. The apricot liqueur is out (tequila replaces it). If it reads dry, add 25 ml agave. Floc fades,
so make bottles to last 1-2 weeks, in the cool room overnight. 8-hour rail limit at room temperature.

### One cocktail
60 ml batch + 30 ml apricot purée + 15 ml lime. Shake hard and short (6-8 s). Single-strain with a
Hawthorne strainer over fresh cubed ice in a rocks glass. About 1.5 standard drinks. The lime matches the
purée's acid (9 g per can); tune it by taste.

**Cost per drink: about $4.09** (tequila $1.79, mezcal $0.88... batch $3.42 + purée $0.21 + lime $0.45).
At $25 (ex GST $22.73): **pour cost 18.0%**, gross profit about $18.64.

## 2. A ROSY INVITATION / A.R.I. ($25)

*Cucumber rum, Lillet Rosé, Cointreau, absinthe, watermelon-rose concentrate, lemon. Fresh, floral & crisp.*
Test build, not yet tasted. Full spec and reasoning: [a-rosy-invitation.md](a-rosy-invitation.md).

- **Cucumber rum (about 680 ml):** 700 ml white rum + 250 g Lebanese cucumber, sliced, peel striped, stem end
  cut off. Cool room, capped, opaque bottle; taste daily, strain at day 2-3 (sieve, then coffee filter);
  use within about 3 weeks.
- **Watermelon-rose concentrate (monthly, 2 bottles H2melon, about 1.15 L, 4 x 250 ml portions):** shake the
  bottle, pour 125 ml off each 1.25 L bottle, freeze capped 24 h (freeze the day you open it, as it keeps 3
  days once opened), thaw upside down over a jug in the cool room 12-20 h, keep the first melt (about 550 ml
  per bottle). Stir in per 100 ml: citric 1.5 g, malic 0.75 g, salt 0.05 g, sorbate 10% solution 0.8 ml,
  rose syrup 5 ml (for 1.1 L: 16.5 g, 8.25 g, 0.55 g, 8.8 ml, 55 ml). No vitamin C. Hibiscus shot only if the
  colour is pale. Label "contains preservative 202". Freeze 250 ml portions; thaw one per week in the fridge.
  Don't filter it hard (the red is in the cloudy particles).
- **Weekly batch (10 drinks, 850 ml, 1 L bottle):** 245 cucumber rum · 195 Lillet Rosé · 150 Cointreau · 12
  absinthe · 250 concentrate. About 23% ABV. Ice well all service, back to the cool room at close; use within
  about a week (untested). Run the overnight haze test first (25 ml concentrate + 60 ml of the spirit mix).
- **One cocktail:** 85 ml batch (60 + 25) + 10 ml fresh lemon. Shake hard, double strain into a chilled coupe.
  About 1.5 standard drinks.

**Cost per drink: about $3.80.** At $25: **pour cost 16.7%**, gross profit about $18.93.

## 3. SANGRIA TINTO ($24)

*Cherry & Spiced Wine Reduction, Brandy, Vodka, Apéritif & Citrus. Fruity, spiced & refreshing.*
Source: [rescued-red-wine-cobblers.md](rescued-red-wine-cobblers.md) (cherry variant, Bright bitters).

### Cobbler batch (per 3 L soft red wine; about 3.3 L at 16-18%)

| Part | Amount |
|---|---|
| Rescued soft red (Pinot, Grenache, Gamay, Merlot, Sangiovese); taste every bottle, bin corked or vinegary | 3 L |
| Spices, simmered from the start | 8 allspice berries, cracked · 15 g fresh ginger, sliced · 1 cinnamon quill · peel of 2 oranges (no pith), then juice those 2 oranges into the pot (150-200 ml) |
| Cherries, added for the last 20 min | 1 kg frozen pitted, or about 2 jars of morello cherries (jar fruit is in syrup, so cut the oleo sugar and taste: unconfirmed) |
| Oleo, off the heat | 36 squeezed lemon halves + 350 g sugar, strained. Never cook the husks |
| Spirit | 700 ml brandy + 700 ml house vodka (200 ml of it for the husk rinse) |
| Bitters, Bright | 30 ml Peychaud's |
| 20% saline | 4 ml |
| Citric | to pH 3.3 or below (about 5 g in the costing; check with a pH strip) |
| Hibiscus, optional (pale or soft reds only) | 10 g dried, off the heat, lid on, 5 min |

1. Simmer the wine with the spices, uncovered; add the cherries for the last 20 min. Reduce until the whole
   pot, fruit juice included, is **1.5 L**.
2. Off the heat: stir in the strained oleo until the sugar dissolves (steep and remove hibiscus first if used).
3. Fine strain, pressing the fruit hard; cool; fine strain again.
4. Husk rinse with 200 ml of the spirit, strain it in, then add the rest of the brandy and vodka, the
   Peychaud's and the saline.
5. Citric to pH 3.3 or below. Bottle, label (variant, bitters, date, "HH 60 / Normal 100").
6. Result about 3.3 L, 16-18%. Safe 2-3 months in the fridge, best within 6 weeks. If it's over 3.5 L the
   pot was under-reduced: add 200 ml vodka (about +1%). Opened bottle: finish within 2 weeks.

The wine's own alcohol mostly cooks off, so the strength is set by the 1.4 L of spirit in 3.3 L.

### Rail bottle (750 ml, 6 pours of 125)
600 cobbler batch · 90 sweet vermouth · 60 Aperol. About 16% ABV, so it **stays on ice** in the well. Fridge,
best within 6 weeks.

### One cocktail (built, the version guests loved)
125 ml bottle pour (100 batch + 15 sweet vermouth + 10 Aperol), a muddled orange slice and lemon slice
with a little lemon juice (15 ml in the costing; write down the real amount), fill with cubes, optional
Coca-Cola top. Happy hour 60 ml batch pour. About 1.6 standard drinks.

**Cost per drink: about $4.35** (batch $0.0277/ml, the brandy and vodka are $70 of the $92 batch).
At $24 (ex GST $21.82): **pour cost 19.9%**, gross profit about $17.47.

## 4. PISCO MA CHÉRIE ($25)

*Pisco, Sherry, Strawberry, Black Pepper & Soda. Tall, juicy & tangy.*

### Strawberry shrub (about 1.1 L, enough for 4 rail bottles)
**Day before:** in a lidded tub in the cool room for 24 h (stir once): 1 kg frozen strawberries · 400 g white
sugar · 200 g demerara · 3 g black peppercorns, coarsely cracked.
**Prep day:** strain, pressing lightly (about 1 L; bin the pulp and pepper). Whisk in until dissolved:
120 ml vinegar (default FORVM Chardonnay; or Woolworths sherry vinegar, or a 60/60 split; check the acidity %
on both labels) · 21 g citric · 7 g malic · 1.5 g salt · optional 5 ml sorbate 10% solution.
**Taste check:** 15 ml shrub + 45 ml cold soda should taste like a bright strawberry soda with no sharp
prickle; if flat, add 3 g citric + 1 g malic (twice at most). Bottle full, label, rest 2 days in the fridge
before batching. Keeps about 3-4 weeks in the fridge (untested); bin it if it fizzes, grows a film or smells
yeasty.

### Batch (700 ml rail bottle, the more-sherry version from the 27 Oct tasting)
270 pisco · 195 Elegante Medium sherry · 235 shrub. About 20% ABV. **Stays on ice.** Best within about 2 weeks;
keep the drum dark in the cool room; never pour leftovers back. (First version: 320 / 140 / 240, about 22%.)

### One cocktail
Highball to the top with cubes. 90 ml batch (a 60 and a 30 jigger; shake the bottle first). Stir 3-4 spins,
pour 75 ml cold soda down the bar spoon, lift once. Garnish: dehydrated orange wheel against the inside of the
glass and one twist of black pepper. About 190 ml, about 9.5% ABV, **about 1.4 standard drinks**. Each
bottle gives 7 full pours. Option: 100 ml pour for 1.6 standard drinks (exactly 7 pours per bottle).

**Cost per drink: about $3.60** (batch $3.39 per 100 ml-equivalent... pisco $2.48, sherry $0.61, shrub
$0.30, soda $0.11, orange wheel $0.10). At $25: **pour cost 15.8%**, gross profit about $19.1. With the
100 ml pour: about $3.98, 17.5%.
