## 1. SUMMER FLING ($25)

*Tequila & Mezcal, Floc de Gascogne, Apricot, Lime & Agave. Fruity, spicy & juicy.*

### Apricot purée (one 825 g can, about 600-650 ml, about 20 drinks)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Canned apricot halves | all the fruit (about 480 g) |
| Can syrup | about 170 g (half): drain all the syrup into a jug, weigh 170 g back into the blender jug, discard the rest |
| Malic acid | 6 g |
| Citric acid | 3 g |
| Salt | 1 g |
| Potassium sorbate, 10% solution | 5 ml |

Blend everything on high for about 1 minute. No sieve. Wide-tip squeeze bottle, labelled "contains
preservative 202", "Made [date] · Use by [date + 10 days]". Vitamin C optional; skip it for the first batch.
Aim for pH 3.5 or below (cheap pH strips). In service, keep the bottle in the esky in ice up to the neck,
top up the ice, and back in the fridge at close; if the ice has gone, don't keep it past that shift. Bin it
if it fizzes, smells yeasty or boozy, or grows mould. Freeze flat in 300 ml bags (10 drinks) on the day you
make it; 2-3 months frozen; thaw in the fridge and never refreeze; fridge days before freezing count toward
the 10. Cans per week = drinks sold / 20.

### Batch (10 drinks, 658 ml, 10 pours of about 65; Ari's amounts, 15 Nov)

| In the bottle | Amount |
|---|---|
| Blanco tequila | 300 |
| Mezcal | 150 |
| Floc de Gascogne | 150 |
| Agave nectar, straight | 50 |
| Chilli bitters | 8 (about 1 dash per pour; taste and adjust) |

About 32% ABV. The apricot liqueur is out. If it reads dry, add 10 ml agave to the bottle. Floc fades,
so make bottles to last 1-2 weeks, in the cool room overnight. 8-hour rail limit at room temperature.

### One cocktail
65 ml batch (a 60 and a 5, or a 65 ml measure) + 30 ml apricot purée + 15 ml lime. Shake hard and short (6-8 s). Single-strain with a
Hawthorne strainer over fresh cubed ice in a rocks glass. About 1.65 standard drinks (more mezcal and Floc than the first build). The lime matches the
purée's acid (9 g per can); tune it by taste.

**Cost per drink: about $4.69** (tequila $1.56, mezcal $1.56, Floc $0.73, agave $0.05, bitters $0.12, purée $0.21, lime $0.45).
At $25 (ex GST $22.73): **pour cost 20.6%**, gross profit about $18.04. At $26: 19.8%.

## 2. A ROSY INVITATION / A.R.I. ($25)

*Cucumber rum, Lillet Rosé, Cointreau, absinthe, watermelon-rose concentrate, lemon. Fresh, floral & crisp.*
Test build, not yet tasted. Full spec and reasoning: [a-rosy-invitation.md](a-rosy-invitation.md).

- **Cucumber rum (about 680 ml):** 700 ml white rum + 250 g Lebanese cucumber, sliced, peel striped, stem end
  cut off. Cool room, capped, opaque bottle; taste daily, strain at day 2-3 (sieve, then coffee filter);
  use within about 3 weeks.
- **Watermelon-rose concentrate (monthly, 2 bottles H2melon, about 1.15 L, 4 x 250 ml portions):** shake the
  bottle, pour 125 ml off each 1.25 L bottle, freeze capped 24 h (freeze the day you open it, as it keeps 3
  days once opened), thaw upside down over a jug in the cool room 12-20 h, keep the first melt (about 550 ml
  per bottle). Stir in per 100 ml: citric 1.5 g, malic 0.75 g, salt 0.05 g, sorbate 10% solution 0.8 ml,
  rose syrup 5 ml (for 1.1 L: 16.5 g, 8.25 g, 0.55 g, 8.8 ml, 55 ml). No vitamin C. Hibiscus shot only if the
  colour is pale. Label "contains preservative 202". Freeze 250 ml portions; thaw one per week in the fridge.
  Don't filter it hard (the red is in the cloudy particles).
- **Weekly batch (10 drinks, 850 ml, 1 L bottle):** 245 cucumber rum · 195 Lillet Rosé · 150 Cointreau · 12
  absinthe · 250 concentrate. About 23% ABV. Ice well all service, back to the cool room at close; use within
  about a week (untested). Run the overnight haze test first (25 ml concentrate + 60 ml of the spirit mix).
- **One cocktail:** 85 ml batch (60 + 25) + 10 ml fresh lemon. Shake hard, double strain into a chilled coupe.
  About 1.5 standard drinks.

**Cost per drink: about $3.80.** At $25: **pour cost 16.7%**, gross profit about $18.93.

## 3. SANGRIA TINTO ($24)

*Cherry & Spiced Wine Reduction, Brandy, Vodka, Apéritif & Citrus. Fruity, spiced & refreshing.*
Based on [rescued-red-wine-cobblers.md](rescued-red-wine-cobblers.md) (cherry variant), changed 15 Nov: Angostura
instead of Peychaud's, plain sugar instead of the lemon-husk oleo, citric acid for sourness.

### Cobber batch (per 3 L soft red wine; about 3.2 L at 16-17%)

| Part | Amount |
|---|---|
| Rescued soft red (Pinot, Grenache, Gamay, Merlot, Sangiovese); taste every bottle, bin corked or vinegary | 3 L |
| Spices, simmered from the start | 8 allspice berries, cracked · 15 g fresh ginger, sliced · 1 cinnamon quill · peel of 2 oranges (no pith), then juice those 2 oranges into the pot (150-200 ml) |
| Cherries, added for the last 20 min | 1 kg frozen pitted, or about 2 jars of morello cherries |
| Sugar, off the heat | 350 g white sugar stirred in until dissolved (jar cherries are in syrup: start at 300 g and taste) |
| Spirit | 700 ml brandy ($43) + 700 ml house vodka ($30) |
| Angostura bitters (Warm pattern) | 30 ml |
| 20% saline | 4 ml |
| Citric acid | start at 10 g. Judge by a finished test drink, not the batch alone (morello cherries are already sour); see the citric test below. Aim for pH 3.3 or below when you have strips |
| Hibiscus, optional (pale or soft reds only) | 10 g dried, off the heat, lid on, 5 min |

1. Simmer the wine with the spices, uncovered; add the cherries for the last 20 min. Reduce until the whole
   pot, fruit juice included, is **1.5 L**.
2. Off the heat: stir in the sugar until it dissolves (steep and remove hibiscus first if used).
3. Fine strain, pressing the fruit hard; cool; fine strain again.
4. Add the brandy, vodka, Angostura and saline. Add the citric acid, then taste.
5. Bottle, label (cherry, Angostura, date, "HH 60 / Normal 100"). About 3.2 L, 16-17%. Safe 2-3 months in the
   fridge, best within 6 weeks. Opened bottle: finish within 2 weeks.

**The lemon-husk oleo is out** (Ari, 15 Nov): it risks bitterness from the pith, and the sugar does the
sweetening on its own. The bitter side comes from the Aperol and the Angostura. If the lemon-peel aroma is
missed, steep the yellow-only zest of 2 lemons (about 16 g) in 200 ml of the vodka for 12-24 h and use that
vodka in step 4. The wine's own alcohol mostly cooks off, so the strength comes from the 1.4 L of spirit.

### 5 L barrel (Ari, 15 Nov): everything except the lemon juice, about 41 pours of 120 ml

| In the barrel | Amount |
|---|---|
| Soft red wine, cooked down to 1.9 L with the spices and cherries | 3.75 L in |
| Spices | 10 allspice berries · 19 g ginger · 1 cinnamon quill · peel and juice of 2-3 oranges |
| Cherries (last 20 min) | 1.25 kg frozen pitted, or about 2.5 jars morello |
| Sugar, off the heat | 440 g (less if jar cherries) |
| Brandy | 875 ml |
| Vodka | 875 ml |
| Angostura bitters | 37 ml |
| 20% saline | 5 ml |
| Citric acid | start at 12 g, taste, add 5 g at a time |
| Sweet vermouth | 600 ml |
| Aperol | 400 ml |

Make the reduction, strain and cool it, then add the rest. About 5 L at about 16% ABV. **The barrel lives in the
cool room.** Each shift, fill a 750 ml bottle from it and stand that in the ice well. Plan on about 4 weeks for
the best flavour (the vermouth and Aperol fade first; untested), and finish an opened barrel within about 2
weeks, as air gets in. If you sell fewer than about 20 a week, make a half barrel.

### One cocktail (Ari, 16 Nov)
Batch here means everything except the fresh fruit: reduction, cherries, sugar, brandy, vodka, Angostura, vermouth,
Aperol, citric acid. **No lemon juice.**

1. 120 ml batch (two 60 ml jiggers) into a tin with a lemon slice, an orange slice and ice.
2. Shake gently (about 5-6 seconds, not hard).
3. Dump everything (liquid, ice and slices) into a tall glass.
4. Top up with soda water if it comes out short: about 45 ml, fixed, so every drink is the same. Coca-Cola
   instead of soda makes it sweeter and spicier, in the kalimotxo style.
5. Garnish a lemon slice and an orange slice with a sprig of mint, dust with ground cinnamon, serve with a straw.

About 1.5 standard drinks, about 200 ml in the glass (about 9-10% ABV). Happy hour pour: 60 ml batch.
**Because the lemon juice is out, raise the citric acid in the batch** (4 L batch: from 10 g to about 30-35 g in
total, in small steps; morello cherries are already sour, so the real need may be lower).
**Citric test (no pH strip):** make a 10% citric solution (5 g in 50 ml water). In a test glass: 120 ml batch + 45 ml soda + ice.
If it tastes flat next to the old lemon version, add 1 ml of the solution (0.1 g) and taste again. Each 1 ml per 120 ml equals about
3.3 g in the 4 L batch. Replacing all 15 ml of lemon juice would take about 9 ml (about 30 g per batch), which is the ceiling.

**Cost per drink: about $4.00** (batch 120 ml $3.48, slices $0.30, soda $0.07, mint and cinnamon about $0.12; spices
in the batch not counted). At $24 (ex GST $21.82): **pour cost about 18.3%**, gross profit about $17.80.

## 4. PISCO MA CHÉRIE ($25)

*Pisco, Sherry, Strawberry, Black Pepper & Soda. Tall, juicy & tangy.*

### Strawberry shrub (about 1.1 L, enough for 4 rail bottles)
**Day before:** in a lidded tub in the cool room for 24 h (stir once): 1 kg frozen strawberries · 400 g white
sugar · 200 g demerara · 3 g black peppercorns, coarsely cracked.
**Prep day:** strain, pressing lightly (about 1 L; bin the pulp and pepper). Whisk in until dissolved:
120 ml vinegar (default FORVM Chardonnay; or Woolworths sherry vinegar, or a 60/60 split; check the acidity %
on both labels) · 21 g citric · 7 g malic · 1.5 g salt · optional 5 ml sorbate 10% solution.
**Taste check:** 15 ml shrub + 45 ml cold soda should taste like a bright strawberry soda with no sharp
prickle; if flat, add 3 g citric + 1 g malic (twice at most). Bottle full, label, rest 2 days in the fridge
before batching. Keeps about 3-4 weeks in the fridge (untested); bin it if it fizzes, grows a film or smells
yeasty.

### Batch (700 ml rail bottle, 7 pours of 100 ml; Ari's 35/35/30, 15 Nov)
245 pisco · 245 Elegante Medium sherry · 210 shrub. About 20% ABV. **Stays on ice.** Best within about 2 weeks;
keep the drum dark in the cool room; never pour leftovers back. (Earlier versions: 320 / 140 / 240, then 270 / 195 / 235.)

### One cocktail
Highball to the top with cubes. 100 ml batch (35 pisco, 35 sherry, 30 shrub: a 60 and a 30 and a 10, or a 100 ml measure; shake the bottle first). Stir 3-4 spins,
pour 75 ml cold soda down the bar spoon, lift once. Garnish: dehydrated orange wheel against the inside of the
glass and one twist of black pepper. About 190 ml, about 9.5% ABV, **about 1.57 standard drinks**. Each
bottle gives exactly 7 pours.

**Cost per drink: about $3.86** (pisco $2.50, sherry $0.85, shrub $0.30, soda $0.11, orange wheel $0.10). At $25: **pour cost 17.0%**, gross profit about $18.87.
