# Signature cocktail pricing: is a flat $26 right? (11 November 2026)

For the conversation with the manager. Market check plus a first costing. **All ingredient costs
below are placeholders based on retail prices**; replace them with invoice (wholesale) prices
before deciding.

  (the Baxter Inn tops out at $28).
- Two drinks become $52.
- The local competition is mostly under $25.

## 2. What each drink costs (placeholder retail prices)

Pour cost = ingredient cost ÷ menu price ex-GST (menu price ÷ 1.1). The usual target is about
**18–25%** (premium cocktail bars about 18–22%). Australian spirits excise is among the world's
highest, and it rose again in 2026, so watch it.

| Drink | Est. cost | Pour cost at $22 | at $24 | at $26 | Price for a 22% pour cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Summer Fling | $5.47 | 27% | 25% | 23% | $27 |
| A Refreshing Indulgence | $4.15 | 21% | 19% | 18% | $21 |
| Pisco Ma Chérie | $4.45 | 22% | 20% | 19% | $22 |
| Olivia (menu version) | $4.79 | 24% | 22% | 20% | $24 |
| Tomato Martini (spec unknown; est. 60 gin + 15 vermouth) | $5.14 | 26% | 24% | 22% | $26 |

Placeholder bottle prices (retail):
- tequila $60, mezcal $80, Floc $40, apricot liqueur $45;
- white rum $40, Lillet Rosé $30, Cointreau $55, absinthe $80;
- pisco $55, Elegante Medium $20, house gin $50, amaretto $35, dry vermouth $25.

Preps are costed from their recipes. Wholesale prices are usually lower, which pushes every
pour cost down.

**Reading it:**
- **Margin isn't the problem at $26.** Every drink is at or under about 23%.
- **The costs differ by drink.** Summer Fling (premium tequila plus mezcal) earns $26. The Reviver
  and Pisco Ma Chérie are healthy at $22–24.
- **The Martini Corner** (draft shows $27; Ari says $23) is mostly spirit, so it costs the most to
  make. Pricing martinis below the signatures pushes guests to the cheaper, stronger drink and
  lowers the margin.

## 3. Proposal: tiered prices, not one flat price

| Tier | Price | Which drinks | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light / sessional | $22 | Simple builds: spritzes, sangria, fizzes, low-ABV | Under the Inner West ceiling; easy second-round order |
| Signature | $24 | Most house-prep drinks (e.g. Reviver, Pisco Ma Chérie, Olivia) | Pour cost about 19–22%; still under city-bar prices |
| Premium | $26 | Premium spirits or complex builds (e.g. Summer Fling) | Justified by cost and craft |
| Martini Corner | $24–26 | Spirit-forward | At or above the signatures, not below |

**Steps:**
1. Cost every drink with invoice prices (this sheet's method).
2. Set each price from the formula: cost ÷ target pour cost (about 20–22%) × 1.1, rounded to the
   tier.
3. Sense-check against what a two-drink visit costs a local ($44–48 rather than $52).
4. Review after 4–6 weeks of sales: drinks that sell slowly at their tier move down a tier, and
   best-sellers can hold the price.

## 4. Update: Sydney price benchmark (ChatGPT research, 12 November 2026)

571 menu records from 62 venues, each with a source link (not individually re-checked here).
Statistics use 445 current, named, full-size drinks. Old and unconfirmed listings are excluded.

| Area | Signature median (P75, max) | Spritz median | Martini median (P75) | Non-alc median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LOCAL (Inner West) | **$23** ($24, max $25) | $21 | $24 ($25) | $16 |
| MIDDLE (Burwood, Concord, Rhodes…) | $23 ($23.50) | $21 | $23 ($25) | $16 |
| CITY | **$26** ($27) | $22 | $25 ($25.25) | $17.50 |

**Key facts:**
- **Flat $26 is above every verified local signature price.** $26 is the city median.
- **Close comparisons:**
  - Pistou (Newtown, French wine bar): house cocktails $24, spritzes $22–23.
  - Piqu: $23–24.
  - Mali Ston (Dulwich Hill): $22–24.
  - Marrickville Tavern: $21.
- **Apéritif & Co's live website** lists signatures at $22–25 and French spritzes at $20–21. A
  flat $26 is a rise of $1–4 a drink.
- **Martinis:** the local median is $24, and Bar Planet (a martini specialist) charges $25–26. The
  draft Martini Corner at $27 would be above the specialist.
- **Tiers are the norm:** 8 of 10 assessable local venues use more than one signature price.
- **Happy hour locally is $15.** Postino Osteria, Summer Hill, runs $15 cocktails Sun–Thu
  5:30–6:30pm. Its regular cocktail prices were not verified.
- **Boothby 2024** (NSW leading bars): average $23.29; CBD $24.29; Newtown $22.51.

**Updated recommendation:**
- Spritzes $20–22.
- Core signatures **$24**.
- Premium signatures and the premium martini **$26**.
- Non-alc $14–17.
- Optional apéritif hour at $15 on selected drinks.

This is consistent with section 3 and the costing above (a $24 core still gives about 19–22% pour
cost on placeholder prices).

**Verified by Ari from the venues' menus (12 November 2026):**
- **Pistou (Newtown):** cocktails $24 (Provençal Paloma, French Negroni, Old Fashioned); spritzes
  $22, Pastis Spritz $23.
- **Piqu (Newtown):** signatures $23–24; classics $19–23; martinis $22–23 (classic martini $23).
