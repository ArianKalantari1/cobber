# Signature cocktail specs for the owner: one cocktail, ingredient by ingredient (14 November 2026)

Per-drink amounts in ml. Costs use retail placeholder prices ex GST (invoice prices to replace them);
no garnish extras, wastage, labour or overheads. A standard drink is 10 g of alcohol (Australia).

## Summer Fling: $25
*Tequila & Mezcal, Floc de Gascogne, Apricot, Lime & Agave. Fruity, spicy & juicy.*

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Blanco tequila | 30 |
| Mezcal | 15 |
| Floc de Gascogne | 15 |
| Agave nectar | 5 |
| Chilli bitters | 1 dash |
| Apricot purée | 30 |
| Lime juice | 15 |

Method: shake hard and short (6-8 s), single-strain over fresh cubed ice. Glass: rocks.
Strength: about 1.65 standard drinks. Cost $4.69; pour cost 20.6%; gross profit about $18.04.
The tequila, mezcal, Floc, agave and bitters come pre-mixed in the batch (about 65 ml per drink).

## Sangria Tinto: $24
*Cherry & Spiced Wine Reduction, Brandy, Vodka, Apéritif & Citrus. Fruity, spiced & refreshing.*

### In the glass, per cocktail

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Cherry and spiced red wine reduction, sweetened | 52 |
| Brandy | 21 |
| Vodka | 21 |
| Angostura bitters | 1 |
| Sweet vermouth | 14 |
| Aperol | 10 |
| Lemon juice | 15 (to confirm) |
| Orange slice and lemon slice | muddled |

Optional Coca-Cola top. Everything except the lemon juice and slices is pre-mixed in a 5 L barrel (120 ml per drink,
two 60 ml jiggers). Glass: to confirm.

### What is in the 100 ml of batch (the recipe is 3 L of wine making about 3.2 L)

| Ingredient | In 100 ml of batch |
|---|---|
| Soft red wine, cooked down (94 ml of wine goes in) | 54 ml of reduction (includes 11 g sugar) |
| Cherries, simmered in then strained out (frozen pitted, or about 2 jars of morello per batch) | about 31 g |
| Brandy | 22 ml |
| Vodka | 22 ml |
| Sugar (350 g per batch) | about 11 g |
| Angostura bitters | about 1 ml |
| Orange peel and juice, ginger, allspice, cinnamon | simmered into the reduction |
| Citric acid | about 0.3 g (more if it tastes flat) |
| 20% saline | about 0.1 ml |

The wine's own alcohol mostly cooks off, so the strength comes from the brandy and vodka: about 16-17% ABV in the
batch. Whole drink: about 1.5 standard drinks. Cost $4.22; pour cost 19.3%; gross profit about $17.60. Brandy $43 and
vodka $30 per 700 ml; the Angostura price is a placeholder. Volumes of the reduction are estimates.

## Pisco Ma Chérie: $25
*Pisco, Sherry, Strawberry, Black Pepper & Soda. Tall, juicy & tangy.*

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Pisco | 35 |
| Elegante Medium sherry | 35 |
| Strawberry and black pepper shrub | 30 |
| Soda water | 75 |

Method: fill a highball with cubes, pour the 100 ml batch, 3-4 spins, soda down the bar spoon, lift once.
Garnish: dehydrated orange wheel and a twist of black pepper. Glass: highball (about 190 ml total).
Strength: about 1.57 standard drinks. Cost $3.86; pour cost 17.0%; gross profit about $18.87.
The pisco, sherry and shrub come pre-mixed in the batch (100 ml per drink).
