# Signature cocktail specs for the owner: one cocktail, ingredient by ingredient (14 November 2026)

Per-drink amounts in ml. Costs use retail placeholder prices ex GST (invoice prices to replace them);
no garnish extras, wastage, labour or overheads. A standard drink is 10 g of alcohol (Australia).

## Summer Fling: $25
*Tequila & Mezcal, Floc de Gascogne, Apricot, Lime & Agave. Fruity, spicy & juicy.*

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Blanco tequila | 30 |
| Mezcal | 15 |
| Floc de Gascogne | 15 |
| Agave nectar | 5 |
| Chilli bitters | 1 dash |
| Apricot purée | 30 |
| Lime juice | 15 |

Method: shake hard and short (6-8 s), single-strain over fresh cubed ice. Glass: rocks.
Strength: about 1.65 standard drinks. Cost $4.69; pour cost 20.6%; gross profit about $18.04.
The tequila, mezcal, Floc, agave and bitters come pre-mixed in the batch (about 65 ml per drink).

## Sangria Tinto: $24
*Cherry & Spiced Wine Reduction, Brandy, Vodka, Apéritif & Citrus. Fruity, spiced & refreshing.*

### In the glass, per cocktail

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Cherry and spiced wine reduction, fortified (the cobbler batch, see below) | 100 |
| Sweet vermouth | 15 |
| Aperol | 10 |
| Lemon juice | 15 (to confirm) |
| Orange slice and lemon slice | muddled |

Method: muddle the slices with the lemon juice, add the batch, fill with cubes; optional Coca-Cola top.
Glass: to confirm. The batch, vermouth and Aperol come pre-mixed in the rail bottle (125 ml per drink).

### What is in the 100 ml of batch (the recipe is 3 L of wine making 3.3 L; each drink is 1/33 of it)

| Ingredient | In 100 ml of batch |
|---|---|
| Soft red wine, cooked down (91 ml of wine goes in; about 45 ml of reduction is left) | 91 ml in, about 45 ml out |
| Brandy | 21 ml |
| Vodka | 21 ml |
| Cherries, simmered in then strained out (frozen pitted, or about 2 jars of morello for the batch) | about 30 g |
| Lemon-husk oleo: sugar | about 11 g (350 g per batch) |
| Lemon-husk oleo: lemon halves | about 1 lemon half (36 per batch) |
| Orange peel and juice | about 1/16 orange (2 per batch), 5 ml of juice |
| Fresh ginger | about 0.5 g |
| Allspice | about 1/4 berry (8 per batch) |
| Cinnamon | about 1/30 of a quill (1 per batch) |
| Peychaud's bitters | about 0.9 ml |
| 20% saline | about 0.1 ml |
| Citric acid | about 0.15 g |

The reduction itself is about 45 ml of the 100, the oleo syrup about 11 ml, the brandy and vodka 42 ml.
The wine's own alcohol mostly cooks off, so the strength comes from the brandy and vodka: about 16% ABV in the
batch. Whole drink: about 1.6 standard drinks. Cost $4.35; pour cost 19.9%; gross profit about $17.47.
Volumes of the reduction and oleo are estimates from the 1.5 L pot and the 3.3 L total.

## Pisco Ma Chérie: $25
*Pisco, Sherry, Strawberry, Black Pepper & Soda. Tall, juicy & tangy.*

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Pisco | 35 |
| Elegante Medium sherry | 35 |
| Strawberry and black pepper shrub | 30 |
| Soda water | 75 |

Method: fill a highball with cubes, pour the 100 ml batch, 3-4 spins, soda down the bar spoon, lift once.
Garnish: dehydrated orange wheel and a twist of black pepper. Glass: highball (about 190 ml total).
Strength: about 1.57 standard drinks. Cost $3.86; pour cost 17.0%; gross profit about $18.87.
The pisco, sherry and shrub come pre-mixed in the batch (100 ml per drink).
