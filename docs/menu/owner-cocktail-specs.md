# Signature cocktail specs for the owner: one cocktail, ingredient by ingredient (14 November 2026)

Per-drink amounts in ml. Costs use retail placeholder prices ex GST (invoice prices to replace them);
no garnish extras, wastage, labour or overheads. A standard drink is 10 g of alcohol (Australia).

## Summer Fling: $25
*Tequila & Mezcal, Floc de Gascogne, Apricot, Lime & Agave. Fruity, spicy & juicy.*

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Blanco tequila | 34 |
| Mezcal | 8.5 |
| Floc de Gascogne | 12.5 |
| Agave nectar | 4 |
| Chilli bitters | 1 dash (about 0.7) |
| Apricot purée | 30 |
| Lime juice | 15 |

Method: shake hard and short (6-8 s), single-strain over fresh cubed ice. Glass: rocks.
Strength: about 1.5 standard drinks. Cost $4.09; pour cost 18.0%; gross profit about $18.64.
The tequila, mezcal, Floc, agave and bitters come pre-mixed in the batch (60 ml per drink).

## Pisco Ma Chérie: $25
*Pisco, Sherry, Strawberry, Black Pepper & Soda. Tall, juicy & tangy.*

| Ingredient | ml per drink |
|---|---|
| Pisco | 35 |
| Elegante Medium sherry | 25 |
| Strawberry and black pepper shrub | 30 |
| Soda water | 75 |

Method: fill a highball with cubes, pour the 90 ml batch, 3-4 spins, soda down the bar spoon, lift once.
Garnish: dehydrated orange wheel and a twist of black pepper. Glass: highball (about 190 ml total).
Strength: about 1.4 standard drinks. Cost $3.60; pour cost 15.8%; gross profit about $19.10.
The pisco, sherry and shrub come pre-mixed in the batch (90 ml per drink).
