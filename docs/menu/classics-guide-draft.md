# Classics, grouped by style (draft for the owner, 2026-10-22)

Same classics, laid out so guests can choose by mood: fresh, bitter or rich. Each group runs from
lightest to strongest, and the existing descriptors are kept. Kept classics: Charlie Chaplin,
Clover Club, Your Choice of Sour, Hanky Panky, Jasmine, Paloma, Old Fashioned and Last Word.
Coffee classics added: Carajillo and Espresso Martini. Leaving: Caipirinha, Chambord Martini,
Espresso Sour, Penicillin, Paper Plane, Boulevardier.

```
CLASSICS

Fresh & Fruity: bright, citrusy and easy to love
  Paloma 24             Tequila, Grapefruit, Lime                 Tall, Citrus, Refreshing
  Clover Club 21 (egg)  Gin, Raspberry, Lemon                     Red Berry, Silky, Sour
  Charlie Chaplin 24    Sloe Gin, Apricot Brandy, Lime            Stone Fruit, Sour, Juicy
  Your Choice of Sour 24 (egg)  Bourbon, Amaretto or Pisco, Lemon Silky, Tart, Moreish

Bitter & Herbal: for Negroni and amaro drinkers
  Jasmine 23            Gin, Campari, Cointreau, Lemon            Bitter, Zesty, Crisp
  Last Word 25          Gin, Green Chartreuse, Maraschino, Lime   Herbaceous, Sour, Strong
  Hanky Panky 25        Gin, Sweet Vermouth, Fernet-Branca        Herbal, Bittersweet, Boozy

Rich & Roasted: coffee and spirit-forward, for after dinner
  Carajillo ––          Licor 43, Espresso                        Roasted, Sweet, Silky
  Espresso Martini ––   Vodka, Coffee Liqueur, Espresso           Roasted, Velvety, Bittersweet
  Old Fashioned 24      Rum or Rye Whisky, Sugar, Bitters         Rich, Smooth, Boozy
```

Notes:
- Groups are 4, 3 and 3, and follow an evening: fresh to start, bitter before dinner, rich after.
- Coffee prices to set. Carajillo written Mexican-style (Licor 43); the Spanish style uses brandy.
  "Vanilla" left out of its descriptors because vanilla is already on three signatures.
- The same groups can carry the Signatures: Watermelon Reviver and Pisco Mon Sherry under Fresh &
  Fruity, White Flower Negroni under Bitter & Herbal, Nutty Old Fashioned under Rich & Roasted.
