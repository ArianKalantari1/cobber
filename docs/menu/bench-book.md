texture, salt, heat or dose. The best moves below are often contrast/texture the

# ★ WAVE 1 — service-optimised (Apéritif & Co, build this week)

Designed for a limited, busy bar: the hard work is prep-side (shelf-stable
bottles made in the quiet); at service it's **≤2 fresh touches, pour-and-go**.

## House Martini (Italicus) — the everyday seller
Floral-citrus. **One batched bottle that pours clean OR dirty — no base swap.**
Spirit of Newtown gin (floral) works here: the **Manzanilla backbone in the batch**
keeps it from tasting too sweet AND makes it dirty-ready.
- **Batch (fridge/freezer, ~11 serves):** 500 ml gin (or vodka) · 175 ml Italicus ·
  75 ml Manzanilla (or dry vermouth) · 150 ml water · 10 drops saline → bottle.
- **Clean (default):** pour ~75 ml straight into a chilled coupe, lemon twist. Done.
- **Dirty (same bottle):** pour 75 ml · +5–10 ml olive brine · olive.
- **Just an olive / a drop of saline:** fine on the clean pour (salt lifts bergamot).
- **The line — swap the Italicus:** Rinquinquin (peach), Briottet (elderflower),
  Le Poirier (pear), quince/rhubarb vermouth.

Dial: if the clean reads too sweet, drop Italicus toward **150 ml** (Ari testing) or
bump Manzanilla to 100. *A full slug of brine would fight a straight Italicus pour —
the Manzanilla backbone is what lets the one batch take the brine cleanly.*

## Maïs Fumé — smoked corn margarita
A savoury, smoky margarita: mezcal + lime + corn cordial (the sweet). **Lapsang
tea carries the smoke — no charring**, so the cordial stays clean gold, not muddy
brown. The cordial holds sweet + acid + salt + smoke; fresh lime brings the Ting.
- **Smoky corn cordial:** 300 g corn (canned/frozen fine) · 300 ml cold-brewed
  lapsang (1 tbsp/300 ml, 2–4 hr, strained) · 250 g sugar · **10 g citric + 5 g
  malic** (2:1 lime-mimic) · 3 g salt → fine-strain. (+ splash high-proof vodka
  for life; freeze spares.) *Dose the lapsang light — taste; it goes ashtray fast.*
- **Batch:** 450 ml mezcal + 50 ml Manzanilla.
- **Service:** 50 ml batch · 30 ml smoky corn cordial · 12 ml fresh lime · short
  shake · rock. **Rim: chilli-lime salt. Garnish: charred kernel** (char is fine
  on a garnish, never in the liquid).
- **Season:** canned/frozen year-round; fresh corn Dec–Feb.

## Solstice — pisco, passionfruit & Manzanilla sour
Double Cobber-validated: pisco × passionfruit **0.42**, Manzanilla × passionfruit
**0.42**. Batched for speed; the Manzanilla dries/lengthens it so it's not just a
sweet passionfruit martini.
- **Honey-pisco batch:** 450 ml pisco · 100 ml honey (shake to dissolve) · 150 ml
  Manzanilla · 200 ml passionfruit liqueur → bottle, fridge (~10 serves).
- **Service:** 90 ml batch · 20 ml fresh lime · shake · coupe. Egg white optional
  (skip when slammed). 3 drops Angostura on the foam if used.
- **Garnish:** dried lime wheel.
- **Fruity variant:** for a riper, more passionfruit-forward drink, add **15–20 ml
  passionfruit purée** at service, drop the batch Manzanilla to 100 ml, + 2 drops
  vanilla. Purée à la minute — never in the batch (perishable).

## Sun-Dried Tomato Martini — the shelf-stable savoury one ("Caprese Martini")
Sun-dried tomato = umami tomato with **no fresh-produce spoilage**; infused into the
spirit it becomes a shelf-stable savoury base. Artichoke Vermouth (in stock) deepens
the savoury-vegetal side; basil is the caprese layer, dialled to taste.
- **Sun-dried tomato gin (prep, keeps months):** 60 g dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes
  per 700 ml **Gin Mare** (or vodka). Steep room temp 4–12 hr (taste) or sous vide
  50 °C / 90 min → strain. *(Oil-packed works — drain well; freeze-filter if it clouds.)*
- **Batch (fridge/freezer, ~10 serves):** 600 ml sun-dried-tomato gin · 100 ml
  Artichoke Vermouth · 100 ml water · 10 drops saline → bottle.
- **Service:** pour ~80 ml, stir if needed. **Garnish:** basil leaf + sun-dried (or
  cherry) tomato; optional parmesan/savoury salt half-rim.
- **Basil layer (optional, à la minute):** a barspoon rich basil syrup **or** a basil-oil
  float — add to taste; keep it small so the martini stays dry (and green syrup out of
  the batch to avoid clouding).
- **Dial:** Artichoke Vermouth's bitterness builds — start 10 ml/serve, push only if you
  want it more aperitivo. Taste it neat first to learn its shape.
- *Taste-driven (umami/savoury), not aroma — no Cobber score; chase seasoning, not a number.*

**Service speed:** House Martini & Sun-Dried Tomato = pour (+ twist/garnish) · Maïs
Fumé & Solstice = 2 pours + shake. All shelf-stable batches, 0–1 fresh touches.

# ★ WAVE 2 — Apéritif & Co new menu (final specs)

Same rules: shelf-stable prep, ≤2 fresh touches, pour/shake-and-go. Allergens
flagged per drink — **verify against real bottle labels before serving.**

## 🍑 Abricot — clarified cognac-apricot sour
Cobber: cognac × apricot 0.35, Manzanilla × apricot 0.385 (strongest stone fruit).
- **Batch (~10):** 400 ml cognac · 150 ml apricot brandy · 150 ml Manzanilla ·
  100 ml honey/sugar syrup → bottle.
- **Service:** 80 ml batch · 22 ml lemon · shake → coupe (no egg; foamer optional).
  Garnish: dried apricot. In-season boost: 15 ml apricot purée or a splash Rinquinquin.
- **Allergens:** sulphites (Manzanilla, apricot brandy, dried-apricot garnish). Use a
  nut-free essence, not apricot-kernel noyau, to stay nut-free.

## 🍍 Ananas — grilled-pineapple rhum (mojito/highball); sister to the Yuzu Mock
- **Grilled-pineapple rhum (prep, months):** char/grill pineapple → infuse 700 ml
  rhum agricole 4–12 hr → strain.
- **Two-bottle batch for shelf life:** A = pineapple rhum + cinnamon syrup (months);
  B = yuzu-lime cordial (yuzu purée + lime super-juice + sugar + citric/malic +
  splash vodka; 2–3 wks + freezes).
- **Service:** ~45 ml A · ~25 ml B · top soda/tonic · crushed ice · **slap mint**
  (don't muddle). Garnish: mint + dehydrated pineapple.
- **Zero-waste byproducts:** rum-pineapple jam (spent flesh + sugar + lemon → months;
  → Pineapple Breakfast Martini), pineapple cordial (juice), tepache/skin-oleo (skins).
- **Allergens:** quinine (if tonic). Otherwise clean, vegan (check jam).

## 🍓 Strawberry — two patterns (shared shelf-stable bases)
Bases: strawberry cordial (purée + sugar + citric/malic + vodka) *or* freeze-dried-
strawberry vodka (40 g / 700 ml, months). **No egg/foam.**
- **Fraise & Poivre (shaken):** 55 ml strawberry vodka · 20 ml strawberry cordial ·
  20 ml lemon · 3–5 drops black-pepper tincture → shake → coupe. Garnish: strawberry
  fan + cracked pepper. *Allergens: very clean.*
- **Strawberry Americano (long aperitivo):** 40 ml strawberry base · 20 ml Aperol
  (or Campari) · 15 ml sweet vermouth · top soda. Optional: milk-clarify the batch for
  crystal-clear. *Allergens: sulphites; carmine (Campari — not vegan); trace dairy
  (clarified version only).*

## 🥃 Confiture — PB&J Boulevardier (brown-butter Maker's Mark)
- **Base (prep, months):** brown 50 g butter → stir into 700 ml Maker's Mark + 40 g
  freeze-dried strawberry → infuse 2–4 hr → freeze, lift fat cap, fine-strain.
- **Batch/build:** 35 ml brown-butter strawberry Maker's · 20 ml Campari · 20 ml sweet
  vermouth · 5 ml PX → stir or freezer-batch (+15% water) → big rock. Garnish:
  dehydrated strawberry + toasted walnut.
- **Allergens: DAIRY (brown butter)**; sulphites + carmine (Campari/vermouth/PX).
  *(Swap fat-wash → peanut = peanut allergen; macadamia = tree nut.)*

## 🥒 Jardin — crisp cucumber & herb refresher (the green/fresh gap)
- **Cucumber cordial (prep, ~2 wks + freeze):** cucumber juice + sugar + citric +
  pinch salt + splash vodka. (Or clarify it, or cucumber-wash the gin.)
- **Build (highball):** 45 ml Hendrick's (or vodka) · 20 ml cucumber cordial · 15 ml
  verjus (or lime) · top soda · dill or tarragon. Garnish: cucumber ribbon + herb.
  *Or up as a shaken gimlet.*
- **Allergens:** sulphites (verjus). Otherwise very clean, vegan.

## 🍋 Vacherin — foamed lemon-meringue pie (dessert)
- **Lemon-curd cordial (squeeze bottle, ~1 month):** peels of 5–6 lemons + 250 g sugar
  (oleo, 2–3 hr) + 120 ml lemon juice + 8 g citric + 4 g malic + 150 g sugar + 1
  vanilla pod + big pinch salt + 40 ml vodka → warm to dissolve, fine-strain.
- **Base batch:** vodka (or brown-butter-washed) + limoncello → bottle (shelf-stable).
- **Service:** 65 ml base · ~20 ml lemon-curd cordial · few drops **foamer** → dry
  shake → wet shake → coupe. Garnish: lemon zest + biscuit crumb / bought meringue kiss.
- **Allergens:** foamer (check for soy/legume); dairy only if brown-butter base. The
  cordial is egg/dairy-free (real jarred curd would add egg + milk).

## 🍫 Dubai — clarified pistachio-chocolate (dessert)
- **Prep:** cacao-nib vodka (60 g nibs / 700 ml, 24 hr, strain); pistachio orgeat
  (pistachios + sugar + water, blend, strain).
- **Batch:** 400 ml cacao-nib vodka (or + 100 ml white crème de cacao) · 150 ml
  pistachio orgeat · sugar to taste · pinch salt → clarify with 250 ml milk + ~40 ml
  citric/lemon (just to curdle) → filter → pale green.
- **Service:** 80 ml over ice or coupe. Garnish: crushed pistachio + dark-chocolate
  shavings / kunafa crumb.
- **Allergens: TREE NUT (pistachio)**; trace dairy (milk-wash); gluten (if kunafa garnish).

- Grilled-pineapple rhum · rum-pineapple jam · pineapple cordial *(Ananas)*

- Black-pepper tincture *(Strawberry, others)*
- Smoky corn cordial (lapsang) *(Maïs Fumé)* · sun-dried-tomato gin *(Tomato)*
- Cross-cutting: citric+malic acid solutions, saline (20%), sugar/honey syrups.

Golden rule: **fresh juice + dairy never go in a long-life bottle** — swap juice for
citric/malic or a super-juice cordial; add dairy/curd/foam à la minute.

# Allergen quick-reference (verify vs labels)

- **Sulphites:** anything wine-based — Manzanilla, vermouth, Aperol/Campari, PX,
  apricot brandy, verjus, dried-fruit garnishes. (Most of the menu — declare broadly.)
- **Dairy:** Confiture (brown butter); Vacherin if brown-butter base; trace in any
  **milk-clarified** drink (Dubai, clarified Americano) — not safe for a real milk allergy.
- **Tree nut:** Dubai (pistachio); Confiture if macadamia fat-wash. **Peanut:** Confiture
  if peanut fat-wash.
- **Egg:** only optional egg-white versions (default builds use none).
- **Gluten:** distilled spirits are gluten-free post-distillation; Dubai's kunafa garnish
  = wheat.
- **Not vegan:** Campari (carmine); honey (Solstice, Abricot).
- **Cleanest drinks:** Ananas, Fraise & Poivre, Jardin.

# 1. Competition — Applejack "Home Turf"

Brief: ≤60 ml Four Pillars gin, prominent; represents Bopp & Tone (post-war
nostalgic-optimism, Mediterranean, "everyday opulence"); make 2× the serve;
present inspiration + method + brand knowledge. Comp 7 Sept 2026, NSW.

## 🥃 GOLDEN AGE (a.k.a. Olivia) — the savoury-golden hero
Olive Leaf gin's own botanicals amplified back into the glass (olive brine echoes
the olive; Brookie's Mac echoes the macadamia; lemon myrtle lifts the citrus).
Cobber: olive brine × Olive Leaf gin **0.19**, kumquat × gin **0.18**,
kumquat × lemon myrtle **0.50** (the lift lever).

- 55 ml Four Pillars Olive Leaf gin
- 22 ml kumquat oleo (raw-caster + salt-pinch version)
- 6 ml olive brine (Sicilian/Nocellara — nutty, not just salty)
- 2.5 ml (barspoon) Brookie's Mac
- 2.5 ml lemon — taste in; keep it sweet-savoury, not sour
- 2 drops lemon-myrtle tincture (or steep 1 dried leaf in the oleo)
- 1 drop saline *(optional — if it needs pop)*
- barspoon honey *(insurance only — if the kumquat bites)*

Short, hard shake → double strain → chilled coupe, up.
**Garnish:** green olive + lemon-myrtle leaf + 3 drops good Aussie EVOO floated +
a toasted macadamia on the saucer.
**2× serve:** 110 gin · 44 oleo · 12 brine · 5 Mac · 5 lemon · 4 drops tincture,
split across two glasses.
**Pitch line:** "Everything in the glass is already in the gin — I amplified Four
Pillars' own olive and macadamia, and used lemon myrtle, the gin's brightest
botanical, to lift the kumquat. Nothing fights, because nothing is a stranger."

## 🌑 AFTER DARK — the dark, smoky counterpoint
- 50–60 ml Four Pillars Bloody Shiraz gin
- 20 ml chocolate-infused Carpano Antica (sweet vermouth)
- 10 ml Pedro Ximénez (PX bridges chocolate 0.38; layer, don't swap)
- 15–20 ml drier charred blood-orange cordial
- 2 dash mole bitters
- 1 drop saline
- smoke from the char (drop peated whisky to keep Four Pillars prominent)

Stir, big block ice. **Garnish:** charred blood-orange, cinnamon smoke.

## ☕ HOME & AWAY — Rare Dry + chai (if you want a warm third)
Chai wants Rare Dry (Olive Leaf is too delicate). Cool-evening / shoulder-season.
- 45 ml Four Pillars Rare Dry gin
- 25 ml chai-milk-washed kumquat cordial (recipe §5)
- 5 ml lemon (whisper)
- barspoon honey *(optional)*
- 2 dash Angostura

Short shake (silky from the wash) or long over block. **Garnish:** dehydrated
kumquat wheel + cinnamon dust.

# 2. Spring / creative menu — full specs

## 🍓 STRAWBERRY-BASIL → 🌶️ FIRE & BLUSH (one batch, two drinks)
A gin build is a **strawberry Southside**; pisco is softer/fruitier. Batch the
shelf-stable parts; keep lime + basil fresh à la minute.

**Batch (per serve):** 45 ml strawberry-infused pisco *or* citrus-forward gin ·
15 ml Cointreau · 10 ml strawberry liqueur (or strawberry syrup).
**10-serve batch:** 450 / 150 / 100 ml. Bottle, fridge. *No citrus, no basil in
the batch — chilli stays out too, so the same well pours both drinks.*

**Strawberry-Basil build (à la minute):**
- 70 ml batch
- 15 ml clear basil syrup
- 22.5 ml lime

Shake, double strain, coupe. Top: 3 drops black-pepper tincture.
**Garnish:** slapped basil leaf / strawberry fan. *(Keep it clear — skip an oil
float; green lives on top as garnish, never blended in, or the colour muddies.)*

**FIRE & BLUSH = same + 7.5 ml Ancho Reyes**, drop basil syrup to 12 ml. Heat
dial 5 ml (whisper) → 10 ml (assertive). Optional chilli-salt half-rim (Tajín, or
2:1 flaky salt : chilli). *Lens: sweet strawberry → green basil → slow smoky heat
(trigeminal + contrast).*

## 🥜 GREEN ON GREEN — pistachio + Olive Leaf gin
Nut-on-nut: pistachio amplifies the gin's macadamia; olive + pistachio + citrus =
Sicily. Saline leans into the savoury.

**A — Silky Sour (easy):**
- 45 ml Olive Leaf gin · 15 ml pistachio liqueur · 20 ml lemon · 10 ml honey
  syrup · 1 drop saline · 1 egg white (or 15 ml aquafaba)
Dry-shake, wet-shake, coupe. Garnish: crushed-pistachio rim · 3 drops EVOO ·
lemon-myrtle leaf.

**B — Milk-Clarified (chef, batchable, clear):**
- Batch 10: 450 gin · 150 pistachio liqueur · 200 lemon · 100 honey syrup ·
  100 water (≈1 L)
- Warm 250 ml full-cream milk → pour the acidic mix INTO the milk → rest 1 hr →
  filter clear. Serve 90 ml over a rock, 1 drop saline/glass.

## 🌷 RHUBARB & ROSE
Gin makes the rose read *intentional* (its botanicals bridge). Cobber: gin ×
grapefruit 0.23; Aperol × grapefruit 0.29 (low-ABV route); pisco × strawberry
0.29.
- 45 ml gin *(see subs)* · 20 ml rhubarb liqueur (or rhubarb cordial / your
  shrub) · 15 ml grapefruit · 10 ml simple, to taste · **1 drop rose extract**
  (or 15 ml strawberry-rose wine syrup — then cut the simple)
Shake, coupe (or over ice). Top: rose-water spritz + 1 dash rhubarb/cardamom
bitters. Garnish: grapefruit twist or dried rose petal.
**Spirit dial:** gin/pisco = fresh-aromatic · Aperol/Lillet = daytime spritz ·
brandy = richer/autumnal · vodka = neutral canvas.
⚠️ Rose EXTRACT ≠ rose water — extract is dosed in **drops**, water in barspoons.

## 🥂 STRAWBERRY-ROSE SPRITZ (uses your homemade syrup; easy Jacaranda stand-in)
- 30 ml gin/vodka · 20 ml strawberry-rose wine syrup · 15 ml lemon · top
  ~90 ml prosecco + splash soda
Build over ice, wine glass. Garnish: strawberry fan + mint/rose petal.
*Red-wine-based syrup → drier/deeper (lean spirit-forward); rosé-based → brighter
(perfect here).*

## 🌸 SOUTHERN FORTUNE — Australian daifuku (Clover Club reconstruction)
Reconstruction logic: gin + strawberry + lemon = Clover Club spine · macadamia
foam = the mochi · strawberry gum = the native soul (Cobber strawberry gum ×
strawberry **0.50**) · daifuku on the plate = the anko + full identity.
- 45 ml gin (citrus-forward or native-botanical)
- 20 ml strawberry-gum strawberry syrup
- 20 ml lemon
- *(optional 1 barspoon egg white for body)*
Shake, fine strain, coupe (clean pink). **Float 20 ml macadamia foam**, dust
strawberry powder, **plate a mini strawberry daifuku** beside.
*Adzuki stays OUT of the liquid — it lives in the daifuku bite (and optionally a
whisper in the foam). See §7 for why.*

## 🎌 ICHIGO DAIFUKU (the cloudy/clear cocktail versions, if not doing Southern Fortune)
**B — Cloudy "Nigori" (easy, no clarifying):**
- 30 ml vodka/rice shochu/gin · 20 ml strawberry syrup · 15 ml amazake · 10 ml
  adzuki syrup · 30 ml rice milk (or 15 cream + 15 water) · 10 ml lemon
Shake, fine strain, up. Garnish: strawberry + salted sakura + optional shiratama.
**A — Clarified "Clear" (dairy only):**
- 30 ml vodka · 20 ml sake · 20 ml strawberry syrup · 15 ml amazake · 10 ml
  adzuki · 12 ml lemon/yuzu · clarify with ~60 ml warm **dairy** milk.
⚠️ Rice milk **cannot** clarify (no casein) — see §7.

## 🎃 SPRING SQUASH — bright pumpkin (shoulder-season / early spring)
Pumpkin as a spring *vegetable*, not a pie: roast for depth, then lift with ginger
+ citrus + sage + a light spirit.
- 45 ml blanco tequila (or gin) · 20 ml roasted-pumpkin cordial · 15 ml lemon ·
  10 ml honey · 2 slices fresh ginger + 1 sage leaf (muddled)
Shake, double strain, coupe. Garnish: sage/edible flower + toasted pepitas.
**Spritz:** 30 ml tequila · 20 ml pumpkin cordial · 15 ml lemon · top soda/
prosecco. *(Wattleseed/smoke pull it to autumn — save those for the winter flip.)*

## 🍧 STRAWBERRY-PISTACHIO CLARIFIED (home for the strawberry-pistachio dust)
- 45 ml gin/vodka · 15 ml pistachio liqueur · 20 ml strawberry syrup · 20 ml
  lemon · 10 ml honey · milk-clarify (~60 ml warm dairy milk)
Serve crystal-clear over a rock or up. **Rim/garnish: strawberry-pistachio dust.**

# 5. Prep library — detailed recipes

### Kumquat oleo (raw-caster + salt)
100 g kumquat (halved, seeds + central pith removed) + 100 g raw caster sugar +
pinch salt. Muddle *gently* (crushing seeds = bitterness), cover, room temp
2–3 hr, agitate every 30 min, strain. *Cut style drives bitterness; sugar type
drives clean-vs-warm; salt suppresses bitter + boosts sweet.*

### Strawberry-rhubarb shrub
250 g rhubarb (red stalks; discard leaves — toxic) + 250 g strawberries + 30 g
ginger + 400 g raw caster. Macerate cold 24–48 hr, stir twice daily, strain (don't
press). Add 350 ml apple cider vinegar + 50 ml white-wine/champagne vinegar. Rest
3–5 days to marry. Keeps 4–6 weeks.

### Clear basil syrup (flavour, not colour — never blend)
Make 1:1 simple (250 g sugar + 250 ml water), cool fully. Add 20–25 g **whole**
basil leaves. **Cold steep** fridge 8–12 hr, taste from 4 hr, strain gently (no
pressing), coffee-filter for clarity. **Sous-vide fast lane:** sealed bag at
55 °C / 30 min → ice bath → strain. Keeps ~1 week (add 10–15 ml vodka to extend).
*Cold/sealed = fresh aroma + least green. Boiling = worst on both.*

### Cherry cordial with agave
500 g pitted cherries + 200 ml agave + 150 ml water, warm 60–70 °C 10 min (don't
hard-boil). Blend, strain (fine sieve + superbag). Off heat: 6 g citric + 2 g
tartaric (or 40 ml lime) + pinch salt. **Almond note (nut-free):** rely on the
cherries themselves first; else a whisper of tonka (grey-area in AU) or a
confirmed nut-free bitter-almond essence, or vanilla + cassia. *Amaretto adds the
note but is NOT reliably nut-safe — keep off allergy builds.* Keeps ~2 weeks.

### Strawberry syrup
250 g strawberries + 200 g sugar → macerate 2 hr or sous vide 55 °C / 45 min →
strain, don't press.

### Strawberry-gum strawberry syrup (native amplifier)
Strawberry syrup + 0.3–0.5 g ground strawberry gum (*Eucalyptus olida*). Steep
10 min, taste, pull. ⚠️ Potent like lemon myrtle — over-steep goes medicinal.

### Adzuki (red bean) syrup
100 g canned sweetened yude-azuki + 100 ml water → warm, blend, push through a
fine sieve → smooth syrup + pinch salt. *Keep it a whisper in drinks (≤10 ml) or
it turns wintry; never in a pale liquid — colour muddies.*

### Macadamia foam (the "mochi")
100 ml macadamia milk + 30 g silken tofu (or 30 ml soy milk, for foam structure) +
pinch salt + 2 drops vanilla + 5 ml simple. Blend smooth, froth with a handheld
milk frother (or iSi, 1 charge). Optional adzuki whisper: 1 barspoon smooth adzuki
syrup (tints warm, keeps anko note in the foam only).

### Cardamom-honey syrup
Honey : hot water 3:1, add cracked green cardamom, warm-steep 15 min, strain.

### Wattleseed syrup
1 tbsp ground wattleseed in 100 ml hot water, steep 10 min, strain, stir 1:1 with
sugar. *Roasty coffee/chocolate — your "smoky" without literal smoke.*

### Chamomile syrup
Steep 2 chamomile tea bags in 150 ml hot water, stir in equal sugar.

### Roasted-pumpkin cordial
Roast pumpkin till caramelised (Maillard depth). Blend with 1:1 sugar syrup to a
thin cordial (not purée), thin with water, strain. Keep it pourable.

### Chai-milk-washed kumquat cordial
Kumquat oleo (from oleo above, or 300 g kumquat + 250 g caster oleo) + 400 ml
strong brewed chai (black tea + cinnamon, cardamom, ginger, clove, star anise) +
15 g citric + 5 g tartaric (or ~40 ml lemon). **Milk-wash:** warm 150 ml full-
cream milk, pour the hot acidic liquid INTO the milk, rest 30 min, filter through
coffee filter till silky/clear. Target ~Brix 24–28, pH ~3.4. Keeps 5–7 days.

### Black-pepper tincture
2 tbsp coarsely cracked black peppercorns + 100 ml high-proof neutral (overproof/
Everclear; vodka works, slower). Steep 24–48 hr, tasting (pepper turns hot fast),
strain into a dropper/atomiser. A few drops or one spray per drink.

### Rice milk (for cloudy daifuku)
**Buy** (Vitasoy / Australia's Own) — easiest, consistent. **DIY (needs blender):**
soak ½ cup raw rice 4 hr (or use 1 cup cooked rice), blend with 4 cups water +
pinch salt (+ 1 tsp sugar), strain through nut-milk bag. Toast the rice first for
a "yaki-mochi" note. *Amazake = buy at an Asian grocer for the best rice flavour.*

### Milk clarification (general method)
Combine all non-dairy ingredients (must be acidic). Warm the dairy milk, pour the
acidic mix INTO the milk (it curdles), rest 30–60 min, filter through coffee
filter/superbag, re-passing cloudy pours until clear. **Only dairy works** (casein
curdles + traps solids). Dairy-free clear = agar clarification instead.

# 7. Technique notes

**Clarification.** Dairy milk clarifies because casein curdles on acid and traps
haze → clear liquid. Rice/oat/almond milk have no casein → they won't clarify;
use them as *body* in a cloudy drink, or use agar for a dairy-free clear.

**Steeping delicate botanicals (basil, mint, elderflower, tea, zest).** Cold and
slow, whole not blended; time makes up for low temperature. Heat drives off the
volatile top notes and pulls chlorophyll. Sous vide (sealed, low temp) is the best
speed/quality trade — the bag traps aroma and blocks oxidation. Robust/roasty
things (wattleseed, spices, coffee) can take heat.

**Colour — red + green = mud.** Complementary pigments blend to brown/grey.
Strawberry red (anthocyanin, bright in acid) + basil green (chlorophyll, browns in
acid) muddies if blended. **Separate by layer:** clean pink liquid, green on top as
garnish/oil-drop. Same logic keeps adzuki out of pale drinks.

**Tannin / lingering finish.** Tea (black = grip, Earl Grey = floral, genmaicha =
toasted rice) adds astringency that coats and lengthens — makes a light drink feel
wine-like. Sous vide low/short to avoid bitterness.

**Flame.** Torched-sugar top (brûléed strawberry) = caramel + sugary lingering;
smoked glass (torch rosemary/cinnamon under an upturned glass) = smoke cling;
flamed citrus peel = caramelised oils.

**Butterfly-pea colour change.** Anthocyanins are pH-reactive: blue → purple →
pink as acid drops the pH. Keep the infused spirit acid-free (stays blue) and add
acid at service. For jacaranda **purple**, dose acid *lightly* — purple is the
midpoint; too much lemon runs it to pink. Store dark + cool; it fades over weeks.

**Rose extract vs rose water.** Extract is concentrated (drops); water is a dilute
hydrosol (barspoons). Overdosing extract = soap. Make a spritz by diluting a few
drops of extract in ~50 ml water.
